About MJ Live

🪑 Sweet Spot
The front-center block closest to the stage — rows A through E. This is the splurge for superfans: you're close enough to catch every spin, kick, and costume detail, and you'll feel the band and the bass in your chest. If being right up front for the moonwalk matters most to you, VIP is the pick.
The main-house center seating — rows F through L — and the Sweet Spot for most guests. The angled upper section toward the rear — rows M through X. Positioned at an angle to the stage rather than dead-center, so you view the show a little more from the side.
