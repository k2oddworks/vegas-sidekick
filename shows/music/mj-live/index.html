What is the show?

What is MJ Live — Michael Jackson Tribute Concert?

MJ Live is a 70-minute Michael Jackson tribute concert at Saxe Theater inside Planet Hollywood. The production combines live vocals, a live band, choreography and recognizable costumes while working through familiar songs from Jackson’s catalogue.

This is concert-style tribute territory rather than Cirque or theatrical biography. Songs such as “Thriller,” “Beat It,” “Smooth Criminal” and “Billie Jean” are the reason for the night, with dancing and stage presentation built around recreating the energy of a Michael Jackson performance.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the entrance to the Saxe Theater?
The Saxe Theater entrance is inside Miracle Mile Shops — the indoor mall that runs through Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino. Do not enter through the hotel lobby looking for a theater marquee. Go into Planet Hollywood, find the Miracle Mile Shops mall entrance, and follow signage to the Saxe Theater inside the mall. Plan to arrive at Planet Hollywood 45 minutes before showtime — seating itself opens about 30 minutes early, but you'll want the extra time to navigate the mall, hit the restroom, grab a drink, and get settled before the house lights go down.
Is MJ Live appropriate for kids?
Yes — MJ Live is a family-friendly tribute concert open to ages 5 and up, and every guest needs a ticket regardless of age (children age 5+ must be accompanied by an adult). It's a high-energy sing-along-and-dance night built around Michael Jackson's biggest hits, so kids who love the music will have a blast. Nothing explicit.
Which seats are best — what's the difference between the zones?
There are three seating categories on the supplied chart. VIP is the orange seating closest to the stage. Preferred is the pink middle section and the Vegas Sidekick Sweet Spot / Our Pick. Standard is the purple section farther back. The chart also marks accessible seating positions near the top of Preferred.
Is it a live band or backing tracks?
MJ Live features live vocals and a live band, which is a big part of why the show feels like a real Michael Jackson concert rather than a lip-sync revue. Powerhouse performances, dazzling choreography, and iconic costumes recreate the magic of Michael's legendary shows.
Can I take photos?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography, and no audio or video recording during the show. There's no outside food or drink (on-site bars are available), and backpacks are allowed as long as everything fits with you in your seat.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
MJ Live is one of Las Vegas' longest-running tribute concerts and runs seven nights a week with professional consistency, so outright cancellations are extremely rare. Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
