The 30-second answer

Is VEGAS! The Show worth seeing?

Yes if “Vegas show” means feathers, dancers, live-stage energy and a little old-school Strip glamour in your head.

VEGAS! The Show leans into the city’s showroom tradition instead of trying to modernize it out of existence. The appeal is the visual scale: costumes, choreography, music and a cast moving across a wide stage. It is easy to follow, runs a tight 75 minutes and works especially well for first-time visitors who want something that actually feels tied to Las Vegas.

Quick fit check

Is this your kind of Vegas show?

The goal here is not to talk you out of it. It is to make sure you are shopping for the right kind of night.

✅ Good fit

  • You want a classic Las Vegas production with dancers, costumes and live-stage energy.
  • You are visiting Vegas for the first time and want something that feels tied to the city.
  • You like a tighter 75-minute show that leaves room for dinner, drinks or another stop afterward.

↔️ Think twice

  • You are actually looking for a magic show, stand-up comedy or a headliner concert.
  • You want a huge modern arena-style production more than old-school showroom Vegas.
  • Your group cares more about a specific performer than the overall production style.

Before you go

VEGAS! The Show FAQ

How much are tickets?

Tickets start at $63. The final total depends on your date and seating section, so compare the live options before booking.

How long is VEGAS! The Show?

About 75 minutes with no intermission.

Where is the Saxe Theater entrance?

Saxe Theater is inside Miracle Mile Shops at Planet Hollywood. Plan extra time to navigate the mall rather than treating the hotel lobby as the theater entrance.

Is the show good for kids?

The show is listed for all ages. It is built around music, costumes, dance and visual spectacle. For very young kids, the 75-minute no-intermission runtime is the practical thing to consider.

What’s the difference between VIP and General Reserved?

VIP covers the main floor rows B–L and gives a straighter look at the stage. General Reserved is farther back and more angled. Because this production uses a wide stage and choreography, I prefer VIP when the price difference makes sense.

When should I arrive?

I’d aim to be at Planet Hollywood about 45 minutes before showtime. That gives you time to find Saxe Theater inside Miracle Mile Shops and get settled without turning the mall into a scavenger hunt.
