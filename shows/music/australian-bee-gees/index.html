What is the show?

What is The Australian Bee Gees Show?

The Australian Bee Gees Show is a 70-minute tribute concert at Thunderland Theater inside Excalibur built around the music, look and stage style of the Bee Gees. The production stays focused on the group’s catalogue rather than expanding into a general disco revue.

Expect the familiar harmonies and disco-era hits to do most of the work, supported by live concert staging and a performance modeled on the original group. It is a straightforward pick for Bee Gees fans who want the songs presented as a full tribute show.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the Thunderland Theater?
The Thunderland Theater is inside the Excalibur Hotel &amp; Casino at 3850 S Las Vegas Blvd. Once you're inside Excalibur, follow signage to the theater. Plan to arrive about 30 to 45 minutes before showtime to park, navigate the casino floor, and get settled before the house lights go down.
Is The Australian Bee Gees Show appropriate for kids?
Yes — the show is open to ages 5 and up, and every guest needs a ticket regardless of age. Guests 18 and under must be accompanied by an adult. It's an upbeat, family-friendly concert of disco-era hits — nothing explicit.
Which seats are best — what's the difference between the sections?
The Thunderland Theater has four sections around a thrust stage. Section 2 — the recommended Sweet Spot — is the center block directly facing the stage with the cleanest sightline. Section 1 sits front and center closest to the stage. Sections 3 and 4 wrap around the sides. Section 2 gives most guests the best balance of proximity and a straight-on view.
Is it a live band?
Yes — The Australian Bee Gees Show performs with a full live band, recreating the harmonies and disco-era sound that made the Bee Gees famous. It's an authentic performance right down to the style, the moves, and the tracks, and it's been seen by more than a million people in over 40 countries.
Can I take photos?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
