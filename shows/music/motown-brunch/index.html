What is the show?

What is Motown Brunch?

Motown Brunch is a Sunday morning show at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort that combines brunch with live Motown music in one sitting.

The format is relaxed and song-driven. Familiar Motown material plays while guests eat, turning the show into a full Sunday-morning plan built around both the meal and the music. The 10 AM start also leaves the rest of the day open for whatever comes next.

The 30-second answer

Is Motown Brunch worth seeing?

Yes if a Sunday morning with brunch and familiar Motown songs sounds like an easy way to start the day.

The concept is simple and useful: sit down for brunch, get a live Motown show at the same time, and still have the afternoon and evening open. It is a daytime Vegas option with a clear reason to exist.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Motown Brunch performed?
Motown Brunch plays at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort, 375 E Harmon Ave — just off the Strip, a short drive or rideshare east of the main action. Alexis Park is a no-casino resort, so it's a quieter, easier-in-and-out venue with free parking. Plan to arrive about 120 minutes before showtime to get seated.
Who is Motown Brunch for?
Yes — Motown Brunch is an all-ages show. It's a feel-good Motown tribute with legendary songs, sparkling costumes, and lots of dancing. Bring the whole family, bring your parents, bring a group of friends — everyone in the room already knows the words.
Which seats are best for Motown Brunch?
The front purple rows put you closest to the stage, center tables keep a straight-on view with table seating, and the turquoise side areas give you a side angle.
Can I take photos during the show?
Still photos may be allowed — check with your usher first before the show starts. As a courtesy to the performers and other guests, avoid flash and video during the performance. When in doubt, ask staff at the door.
What are the showtimes?
Motown Brunch runs Sunday mornings at 10:00 AM. Sundays only. Always confirm the exact date and time at checkout before you book.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a production cancellation, you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
