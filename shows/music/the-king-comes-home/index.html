About The King Comes Home

His legend lives on. Celebrate the King of Rock 'n' Roll in a powerful live tribute to Elvis Presley's unforgettable music and legacy.

The King Comes Home brings Elvis's biggest hits to life with powerhouse vocals and live music — the songs, the swagger, and the showmanship that made him a global icon. It plays in the Westgate Cabaret, an intimate and cozy theater where you're close to the stage and wrapped in the music. There's a warmth to a room this size that a giant arena can't match: you feel like you're right there with the King.

Ninety minutes of the songs that made Elvis a legend — from the early rock 'n' roll firestarters to the sweeping Vegas-era ballads and gospel-tinged anthems. It's a proper setlist, delivered with the swagger, the moves, and the charisma that defined the King. Come ready to sing along.
🎸 Powerhouse Vocals & Live Music
This is a live tribute concert, not a soundtrack playback. Powerhouse vocals and live musicianship recreate the sound and feel of an Elvis show, so it plays like the real thing. The kind of performance that reminds you exactly why Elvis packed showrooms night after night.
🎟️ An Intimate, Cozy Room
The King Comes Home plays in the Westgate Cabaret — a warm, intimate theater rather than a cavernous arena. You're close to the stage, the sound wraps around you, and there's a real connection with the performer. It's the kind of room where every seat feels like a good one.
