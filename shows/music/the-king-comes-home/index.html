What is the show?

What is The King Comes Home?

The King Comes Home is a 90-minute Elvis Presley tribute concert at Westgate Las Vegas. The production centers on live music, strong vocals and the familiar songs associated with Elvis rather than using his catalogue as only one part of a broader Vegas-history show.

That focus gives the night a concert feel. The point is to spend a full set inside the Elvis songbook and stage persona, making this a straightforward choice for visitors who want a dedicated King of Rock ’n’ Roll tribute.

Frequently asked questions

Where is The King Comes Home performed?
The King Comes Home plays at the Westgate Cabaret inside Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino, 3000 Paradise Rd, just off the Strip near the Las Vegas Convention Center. The Westgate is a fitting home for an Elvis tribute — it's the very resort where Elvis Presley performed his legendary Las Vegas residency. Once you're at the resort, follow signage to the theater. Plan to arrive early to park, grab a drink, and get settled before the show begins.
Is The King Comes Home family-friendly?
Yes — it's an Elvis Presley tribute concert open to ages 5 and up, celebrating his music and legacy. It's the kind of feel-good, sing-along show that plays well across generations, from lifelong Elvis fans to kids discovering the King's hits for the first time. Nothing explicit.
What kind of show is it, and what's the theater like?
It's a live tribute concert to Elvis Presley — powerhouse vocals, live music, and the King's biggest hits. It plays in the Westgate Cabaret, an intimate and cozy theater rather than a giant arena, so you're close to the stage and wrapped in the music. Warm, up-close, and full of the songs that defined rock 'n' roll.
When should I arrive?
Showtimes vary by day — Tuesday at 5:30 PM, Wednesday through Friday at 7:30 PM, and Saturday at 2:00 PM — so double-check your date at checkout. Plan to arrive 30 to 45 minutes early to park at the Westgate, find the theater, grab a drink, and get settled before the show starts.
Can I take photos?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography. It's a concert-style tribute, so soak up the atmosphere and sing along.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
