What is the show?

What is Sinatra Live!?

Sinatra Live! is a Frank Sinatra tribute at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort starring Michael Sinatra. The show leans into the Great American Songbook and Rat Pack-era sound in a smaller, more intimate setting than a large Strip production.

This is a song-first tribute rather than a biography or impersonator spectacle. Familiar standards, classic vocal styling and the mood of the Sinatra era are the reason to book it, especially for guests who want that catalogue without a giant production around it.

The 30-second answer

Is Sinatra Live! worth seeing?

Yes if you want a smaller, song-first Frank Sinatra tribute centered on the Great American Songbook rather than a large Vegas production.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Sinatra Live! performed?
Sinatra Live! plays at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort, 375 E Harmon Ave — just off the Strip, a short drive or rideshare east of the main action. Alexis Park is a no-casino resort, so it's a quieter, easier-in-and-out venue with free parking. Plan to arrive about 30 minutes before showtime to get seated.
Who is Sinatra Live! for?
Yes — Sinatra Live! is an all-ages show. It's a feel-good Motown tribute with legendary songs, sparkling costumes, and lots of dancing. Bring the whole family, bring your parents, bring a group of friends — everyone in the room already knows the words.
Which seats are best for Sinatra Live!?
The front rows put you closest to the stage, the center tables give you a straight-on view with table seating, and the side seating gives you a side angle.
Can I take photos during the show?
Still photos may be allowed — check with your usher first before the show starts. As a courtesy to the performers and other guests, avoid flash and video during the performance. When in doubt, ask staff at the door.
What are the showtimes?
Sinatra Live! runs Monday through Wednesday plus Friday, Saturday and Sunday at 7:30 PM. Dark Thursday. Always confirm the exact date and time at checkout before you book.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a production cancellation, you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
