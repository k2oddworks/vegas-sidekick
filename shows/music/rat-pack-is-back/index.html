What is the show?

What is The Rat Pack Is Back!?

The Rat Pack Is Back! is a 75-minute tribute concert at Tuscany Suites & Casino celebrating Frank Sinatra, Dean Martin and Sammy Davis Jr. The show combines their familiar songs with live big-band sound and the easy comedy-and-banter style associated with the Rat Pack era.

Instead of focusing on only one singer, the production recreates the chemistry of the trio as part of the appeal. It is built for guests who want the music and nightclub atmosphere of classic Las Vegas in one compact show.

The 30-second answer

Is The Rat Pack Is Back! worth seeing?

Yes if you want the Sinatra, Dean Martin and Sammy Davis Jr. songbook with a live big-band feel and Rat Pack-style banter.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the Copa Room at Tuscany Suites & Casino?
Tuscany Suites & Casino is at 255 E Flamingo Rd, just east of the Strip near the Boulevard Pool corridor. The Copa Room is inside the resort — follow signage from the lobby. Plan to arrive at least 30–45 minutes before showtime, since tickets here are picked up at the box office rather than delivered instantly (see below).
Is The Rat Pack Is Back! appropriate for kids?
All guests must be 12 years of age or older — every guest needs a ticket regardless of age. It's a big-band tribute concert with some adult-leaning banter and cocktail-lounge humor, so it plays better for tweens and up than for young children.
Do I need to pick up my tickets at the box office?
Yes — this is one of the few shows we cover where tickets are not an instant email download. Your tickets must be picked up in person at the Tuscany box office before the show. Build in extra time before your showtime to collect them.
How do VIP and General Admission compare at the Copa Room?
VIP includes every seating area in front of General Admission, including the seats on both sides. General Admission occupies the four rear rows. The Copa Room is a cozy showroom, so both categories keep the stage comfortably in view.
When should I arrive?
Arrive at least 30–45 minutes before the 7:30 PM curtain — you'll need time to pick up your tickets at the box office, find the Copa Room, and get seated. This show doesn't use instant ticket delivery, so don't plan to walk in right at showtime.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation, you'll be notified and given refund options. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
