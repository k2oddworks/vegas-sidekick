What is Donny Osmond — DONNY?

Donny Osmond’s Las Vegas show is a 90-minute song-and-dance production at Harrah’s Showroom built around his music, stage presence and long career. The set blends familiar catalogue material with newer music instead of functioning as a pure nostalgia concert.

The production uses a full Vegas-show approach—music, choreography and polished staging—while keeping Osmond himself at the center. If seeing Donny is the reason you are considering the ticket, this is the resident-show version of that experience rather than a tribute or retrospective built around someone else.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Harrah's Showroom?
Harrah's Showroom is inside Harrah's Las Vegas at 3475 S Las Vegas Blvd, right on the Strip between The LINQ and Flamingo. Follow signage through the casino to the showroom. Plan to arrive about 30 to 45 minutes early to park, navigate the casino floor, and get settled before the house lights go down.
Is the Donny Osmond show appropriate for kids?
Yes — there are no age restrictions. It's a high-energy musical spectacular blending nostalgic hits with new music, suitable for the whole family. Nothing explicit.
Which seats are best?
Rows K through O on the right and KK through OO on the left are the Vegas Sidekick Sweet Spot / Our Pick. Sections AAA, A and AA sit closest to the stage. Rows P and PP are farther back at tables, while Q and QQ are the rearmost booth rows.
What should I expect from the show?
Donny Osmond's solo Las Vegas show is a completely reimagined song-and-dance experience — a side of Donny audiences haven't seen before. He blends the timeless, nostalgia-inducing hits fans know and love with fresh new music and stunning dancing, all in a high-voltage 90-minute production.
Can I take photos or use my phone during the show?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography, and guests are kindly asked to refrain from using cell phones during the performance.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
