What is the show?

What is Queen Selena?

Queen Selena is a tribute to Selena Quintanilla at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort. The production centers on Selena’s music, wardrobe and recognizable performance style instead of broadening into a larger Latin-music revue.

That focused approach is what makes the show work for fans. The songs and stage presentation carry the night, while the smaller off-Strip showroom keeps it closer to a tribute concert than a giant Las Vegas spectacular.

The 30-second answer

Is Queen Selena worth seeing?

Yes if Selena’s music, wardrobe and stage style are the whole reason for the night and you want a focused tribute rather than a broader Latin-music revue.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Queen Selena performed?
Queen Selena plays at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort, 375 E Harmon Ave — just off the Strip, a short drive or rideshare east of the main action. Alexis Park is a no-casino resort, so it's a quieter, easier-in-and-out venue with free parking. Plan to arrive about 30 minutes before showtime to get seated.
Who is Queen Selena for?
Yes — Queen Selena is an all-ages show. It's a feel-good Motown tribute with legendary songs, sparkling costumes, and lots of dancing. Bring the whole family, bring your parents, bring a group of friends — everyone in the room already knows the words.
Which seats are best for Queen Selena?
The front rows put you closest to the stage, the center tables give you a straight-on view with table seating, and the side seating gives you a side angle.
Can I take photos during the show?
Still photos may be allowed — check with your usher first before the show starts. As a courtesy to the performers and other guests, avoid flash and video during the performance. When in doubt, ask staff at the door.
What are the showtimes?
Queen Selena runs Thursday through Sunday at 9:00 PM. Dark Monday through Wednesday. Always confirm the exact date and time at checkout before you book.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a production cancellation, you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
