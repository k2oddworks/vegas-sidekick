What is the show?

What is Barry Manilow’s Las Vegas show?

Barry Manilow: The Hits Come Home! is a 90-minute residency at the International Theater inside Westgate Las Vegas built around Manilow himself and the songs that made his career. This is the actual headliner performing his own catalogue, not a tribute act or a theatrical production using his music.

Expect a full concert presentation with familiar songs, live musicians and classic Vegas headliner energy. The format is deliberately direct: if “Mandy,” “Copacabana,” “Can’t Smile Without You” and the rest of the Manilow songbook are why you are considering the ticket, the show keeps the focus there rather than surrounding it with a separate story or spectacle.

The 30-second answer

Is Barry Manilow worth seeing in Vegas?

If Barry Manilow is the reason you're looking at this page, this is an easy show to understand: a career-spanning concert in a room built around him, with the familiar songs doing most of the work.

This isn't a Cirque-style production where the set is the star, and it isn't a tribute act. The draw is Manilow himself, his catalog, a full concert presentation and a crowd that knows exactly what it came for.

🌵 Kris Kidd's take

I'd sell this on certainty, not hype. You already know whether “Mandy,” “Copacabana” and “Can't Smile Without You” sound like your night. If they do, the Westgate run gives you the real thing.

Why book it

A genuine Vegas headliner residency with a catalog people recognize before they sit down.

Good to know

Westgate sits off the main Strip corridor, and Manilow performs in date blocks rather than nightly. It takes a little more planning than a center-Strip show.
