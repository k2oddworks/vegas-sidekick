What is the show?

What is Barry Manilow’s Las Vegas show?

Barry Manilow: The Hits Come Home! is a 90-minute headliner residency at the International Theater inside Westgate Las Vegas. Barry Manilow performs his own catalog, backed by a full concert presentation and live musicians.

The concert is centered on the music people already associate with him, including “Mandy,” “Copacabana” and “Can’t Smile Without You,” with a full concert presentation and live musicians. It plays in select performance blocks rather than on a fixed nightly schedule, so the first thing to check is whether your Vegas dates line up with one of his Westgate performances.

The 30-second answer

Is Barry Manilow worth seeing in Vegas?

Yes if Barry Manilow himself is the reason you are shopping. You get the headliner, the familiar catalog and a full traditional Vegas concert.

This is one of the easier show decisions in town. If those songs already mean something to you, the show does not need a complicated premise to justify the ticket.

Where does Barry Manilow perform in Las Vegas?

Barry Manilow performs at the International Theater inside Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino at 3000 Paradise Road.

How long is Barry Manilow's Las Vegas show?

The show runs about 90 minutes.

When does Barry Manilow perform at Westgate?
