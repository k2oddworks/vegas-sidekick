Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Where is Bugsy's Cabaret at the Flamingo?
Bugsy's Cabaret is an intimate showroom inside the Flamingo Las Vegas at 3555 S Las Vegas Blvd, just off the casino floor. Plan to arrive about 45 minutes before showtime to park, walk in, hit the restroom, grab a drink, and get settled before Wayne takes the stage at 7:00 PM.
Is Wayne Newton's show appropriate for kids?
Yes. Everyone must be 5 years of age or older, and the show is family-friendly — crowd-favorite songs, stories from a lifetime in Las Vegas, a live band, and an audience Q&A. No adult content.
What's included with a VIP Reserved ticket?
A VIP Reserved ticket includes a Wayne Newton Meet & Greet — a chance to meet Mr. Las Vegas in person. General Admission is open seating within your section, and because Bugsy's Cabaret is such a small, intimate room, there really isn't a bad seat in the house.
How long is Wayne Newton: Up Close and Personal?
The show runs about 80 minutes with no intermission. Wayne sings crowd favorites, tells stories from more than 50 years on the Strip, plays several of the instruments he's mastered with a live band, and takes questions from the audience.
Can I take photos during the show?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the rare event of a cancellation, you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
