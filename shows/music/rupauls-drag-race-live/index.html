Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know RuPaul's Drag Race to enjoy the show?
Not at all. The show is designed to work for first-timers and die-hard fans equally. If you know the franchise you'll catch the references and feel right at home. If you don't, you're still watching world-class performers do what they do — the entertainment speaks for itself.
Which queens will be performing on my date?
The lineup rotates and is subject to change, so specific queens on any given night aren't guaranteed in advance. The show features queens from the RuPaul's Drag Race roster including Asia O'Hara, Jaida Essence Hall, Derrick Barry, Shannel, Kyle Sonique Love, and Jewels Sparkles — but the exact performers vary. Check closer to your date for any announcements.
Is it appropriate for kids?
There is no age restriction. That said, this is a high-energy adult entertainment show with suggestive humor, bold costuming, and adult-oriented comedy. Most families with older kids and teenagers do fine — use your own judgment based on what your family is comfortable with.
Where do I pick up my tickets?
Tickets are delivered to your email immediately after purchase and accessed digitally on your phone at the door. No will-call required. The Flamingo Showroom is located inside Flamingo Las Vegas at 3555 S Las Vegas Blvd, center Strip.
Is there parking at the Flamingo?
Yes — Flamingo Las Vegas has a parking garage. Rideshare drop-off is available on Flamingo Road. The property is also connected to the LINQ Promenade and within walking distance of several other Strip properties if you're coming from nearby.
Can I get a refund if my plans change?
Refunds are available for exceptional circumstances such as the unlikely event that a show cancels. Check the full terms and conditions before purchasing.
