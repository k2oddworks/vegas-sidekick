What is the show?

What is Piano Man — A Tribute to Elton John & Billy Joel?

Piano Man is a 75-minute tribute concert at V3 Theater inside Planet Hollywood built around the music of Elton John and Billy Joel. Broadway performer Kyle Martin leads the show at the piano with a live band and dancers, so the night covers two major catalogues instead of focusing on only one artist.

The format is concert-first: familiar songs, live playing and stage energy are the point. It works especially well for groups split between Elton John and Billy Joel because the show turns that disagreement into the premise rather than making you choose one tribute over the other.

Frequently asked questions

Is Piano Man appropriate for kids?
Yes — there are no age restrictions. It's a high-energy tribute concert built around Elton John and Billy Joel's biggest hits, with nothing explicit. Families and multi-generational groups are welcome, especially fans who grew up on this music.
What's the difference between VIP and General Admission?
VIP is the orange section closest to the stage. General Admission is the purple section directly behind it. The theater is narrow, so both sections stay fairly centered on the stage; the main difference is how close you want to sit.
Where do I enter the V3 Theater and when should I arrive?
The V3 Theater is inside Miracle Mile Shops at Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino, upstairs from the V Theater — enter through the mall, not the hotel lobby. Plan to arrive at Planet Hollywood about 45 minutes early to find the theater, hit the restroom, grab a drink, and get settled before the lights go down.
What should I expect from the show?
Broadway star Kyle Martin fronts a full tribute to Elton John and Billy Joel — Tiny Dancer, Rocket Man, Uptown Girl, My Life, and 15 more, backed by a live band and dancers. It's a sing-along, high-energy show built to have the whole room dancing by the end.
Can I take photos during the show?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography or video recording during the performance.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Piano Man runs seven nights a week with consistent scheduling, so outright cancellations are rare. Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation, you'll be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms at checkout before purchasing.
