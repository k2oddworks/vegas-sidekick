Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Where is Sir Elton performed?
Sir Elton plays at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort, 375 E Harmon Ave — just off the Strip, a short drive or rideshare east of the main action. Alexis Park is a no-casino resort, so it's a quieter, easier-in-and-out venue with free parking. Plan to arrive about 30 minutes before showtime to get seated.
Who is Sir Elton for?
Yes — Sir Elton is an all-ages show. It's a feel-good Motown tribute with legendary songs, sparkling costumes, and lots of dancing. Bring the whole family, bring your parents, bring a group of friends — everyone in the room already knows the words.
Which seats are best for Sir Elton?
The tables in the center of the showroom are the Sweet Spot. You're close to the stage, you've got room for a drink, and you're perfectly placed to get up and dance — which this room makes easy. The Athena reserved blocks put you right in front of the stage, side Booths (1–4) are a tucked-in option for small groups, and General Admission at the back is the value pick.
Can I take photos during the show?
Still photos may be allowed — check with your usher first before the show starts. As a courtesy to the performers and other guests, avoid flash and video during the performance. When in doubt, ask staff at the door.
What are the showtimes?
Sir Elton runs Monday and Tuesday at 8:00 PM. Dark Wednesday through Sunday. Always confirm the exact date and time at checkout before you book.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a production cancellation, you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
