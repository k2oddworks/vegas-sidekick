What is the show?

What is King of Diamonds?

King of Diamonds is a Neil Diamond tribute at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort. The show is built around the songs people came to hear—music like “Sweet Caroline” and “Cracklin’ Rosie”—rather than a broad oldies revue that only spends a few minutes on Neil Diamond.

The smaller showroom keeps the focus on the catalogue and the performance instead of giant production effects. If your group wants a sing-along-friendly tribute night centered on one artist, that is exactly what this show is trying to deliver.

Frequently asked questions

Where is King of Diamonds performed?
King of Diamonds plays at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort, 375 E Harmon Ave — just off the Strip, a short drive or rideshare east of the main action. Alexis Park is a no-casino resort, so it's a quieter, easier-in-and-out venue with free parking. Plan to arrive about 30 minutes before showtime to get seated.
Who is King of Diamonds for?
Yes — King of Diamonds is an all-ages show. It's a feel-good Motown tribute with legendary songs, sparkling costumes, and lots of dancing. Bring the whole family, bring your parents, bring a group of friends — everyone in the room already knows the words.
Which seats are best for King of Diamonds?
The front rows put you closest to the stage, the center tables give you a straight-on view with table seating, and the side seating gives you a side angle.
Can I take photos during the show?
Still photos may be allowed — check with your usher first before the show starts. As a courtesy to the performers and other guests, avoid flash and video during the performance. When in doubt, ask staff at the door.
What are the showtimes?
King of Diamonds runs Monday through Wednesday plus Sunday at 9:00 PM. Dark Thursday, Friday and Saturday. Always confirm the exact date and time at checkout before you book.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a production cancellation, you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
