Is Wastin' Away worth seeing?

A poolside Jimmy Buffett tribute at Alexis Park Resort, Las Vegas. "Margaritaville," "Cheeseburger in Paradise" and the rest of the Parrothead songbook, three nights a week just off the Strip.

Wastin’ Away is a poolside Jimmy Buffett tribute at Alexis Park Resort. "Margaritaville," "Cheeseburger in Paradise," "Fins," "Come Monday" — the Parrothead songbook, played poolside for a crowd that came to have a good time.

Buffett's catalogue has never really been about the songs so much as the mood, and a resort with a pool and no casino floor turns out to be a better fit for that than a Strip theatre. An early 6:30 PM start makes it a comfortable pre-dinner show.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Wastin' Away performed?
Wastin' Away is a poolside show at Alexis Park Resort, 375 E Harmon Ave — just off the Strip, a short drive or rideshare east of the main action. Alexis Park is a no-casino resort, so it's a quieter, easier-in-and-out venue with free parking. Plan to arrive about 30 minutes before showtime to get settled in.
Who is Wastin' Away for?
Wastin' Away is an all-ages poolside show for anyone who likes Jimmy Buffett's music and a relaxed, sing-along atmosphere. It suits couples, groups of friends, and families alike — the poolside setting keeps things casual.
Which seats are best for Wastin' Away?
Wastin' Away is poolside general admission — there's no assigned seating chart. Arrive a little early to grab a spot close to the stage; poolside lounge seating fills in around the performance area as showtime approaches.
Can I take photos during the show?
Still photos may be allowed — check with your usher first before the show starts. As a courtesy to the performers and other guests, avoid flash and video during the performance. When in doubt, ask staff at the door.
What are the showtimes?
Wastin' Away runs Monday, Tuesday and Sunday at 6:30 PM. Dark Wednesday through Saturday. Always confirm the exact date and time at checkout before you book.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a production cancellation, you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
