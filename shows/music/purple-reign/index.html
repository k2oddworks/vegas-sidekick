What is Purple Reign — The Prince Tribute Show?

Purple Reign is a 75-minute Prince tribute concert at V Theater inside Planet Hollywood. A live band, familiar songs and a performance styled around Prince’s sound and stage presence keep the show tightly focused on one artist rather than turning it into a general ’80s revue.

The catalogue is the main attraction, so expect the night to move through recognizable Prince material with concert energy rather than a biography or theatrical storyline. If you are booking because you want Prince music performed live in Vegas, that is exactly the lane.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the entrance to the V Theater?
The V Theater entrance is inside Miracle Mile Shops — the indoor mall that runs through Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino — not through the hotel's main casino entrance. Go into Planet Hollywood, find the Miracle Mile Shops mall entrance, and follow signage to the V Theater inside the mall. Plan to arrive at Planet Hollywood 45 minutes before showtime so you have time to navigate the mall, hit the restroom, grab a drink, and get settled before the house lights go down.
Is Purple Reign appropriate for kids?
No — Purple Reign has an age restriction. Guests must be 16 years of age or older. It's a high-energy tribute concert, not a family show.
Which seats are best — what's the difference between the zones?
VIP and Preferred are the Vegas Sidekick Sweet Spot / Our Pick. VIP wraps closest to the stage. Preferred is the centered section directly behind it. General Admission is split into separate left and right side sections; both are the same seating category on the supplied chart.
Is it a live band or backing tracks?
Purple Reign performs with a live band, which is a big part of why the tribute lands the way it does. The show features Jason Tenner or Donelvan Thigpen as Prince, both award-winning performers whose voices and stage presence have been matched to the icon in painstaking detail.
Can I take photos?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
