About Purple Reign

Party like it's 1999. Purple Reign: The Prince Tribute Show returns you to the era of blistering performances that defined The Artist.

This award-winning tribute features Jason Tenner or Donelvan Thigpen as Prince — with countless years of experience, both performers have matched the icon's voice and stage presence to perfection. People are consistently amazed at the vocal and physical similarities. Backed by a full live band, Purple Reign delivers an exhilarating performance that faithfully captures the sound, the style, and the swagger of one of music's most singular stars, all in 75 electric minutes at V Theater inside Miracle Mile Shops.
