What is the show?

What is All Motown?

All Motown is a Las Vegas Motown tribute led by the all-female Duchesses of Motown at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort. The show centers on vocal performances, coordinated stage presentation and the familiar sound of classic Motown rather than trying to cover every era of oldies music.

The songbook reaches into music associated with acts including The Supremes, Martha and the Vandellas, Gladys Knight, Mary Wells and The Marvelettes. That makes this a focused pick for people who specifically want the Motown sound, with a compact 75-minute runtime in a smaller off-Strip showroom.

Yes if you want a Motown-focused tribute night led by an all-female vocal group rather than a broad oldies revue.

Frequently asked questions

Where is All Motown performed?
All Motown plays at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort, 375 E Harmon Ave — just off the Strip, a short drive or rideshare east of the main action. Alexis Park is a no-casino resort, so it's a quieter, easier-in-and-out venue with free parking. Plan to arrive about 30 minutes before showtime to get seated.
Is All Motown family friendly?
Yes — All Motown is an all-ages show. It's a feel-good Motown tribute with legendary songs, sparkling costumes, and lots of dancing. Bring the whole family, bring your parents, bring a group of friends — everyone in the room already knows the words.
Which seats are best for All Motown?
The tables in the center of the showroom are the Sweet Spot. You're close to the stage, you've got room for a drink, and you're perfectly placed to get up and dance — which this show absolutely wants you to do. The Athena reserved blocks put you right in front of the stage, side Booths (1–4) are a tucked-in option for small groups, and General Admission at the back is the value pick.
Can I take photos during the show?
Still photos may be allowed — check with your usher first before the show starts. As a courtesy to the performers and other guests, avoid flash and video during the performance. When in doubt, ask staff at the door.
What are the showtimes?
All Motown runs Tuesday through Thursday plus Saturday and Sunday at 7:30 PM. Dark Monday and Friday. Always confirm the exact date and time at checkout before you book.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a production cancellation, you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
