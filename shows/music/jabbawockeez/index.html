Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Where is the Jabbawockeez Theater at MGM Grand?
The Jabbawockeez Theater is located inside MGM Grand on the Las Vegas Strip. The theater entrance is near the main casino floor — look for Jabbawockeez signage once you're inside the resort. MGM Grand is at 3799 S Las Vegas Blvd. Plan to arrive 20-30 minutes before showtime to allow time to navigate the resort, find your seats, and get settled.
Is Jabbawockeez appropriate for kids?
Yes — Jabbawockeez is genuinely great for families. Children 4 and older require their own ticket. Children 3 and under can be admitted as a lap seat at no charge. Anyone under 18 must be accompanied by an adult. The show is high-energy, visually spectacular, and funny — kids who like dancing, music, or anything that moves fast will love it. There's no adult content.
What are Category B and C seats, and why does Spike recommend them?
Category B and C are the mid-theater sections that offer the best combination of proximity to the stage and a clear full-stage view. Category A seats are closest to the stage but can be too close for the full choreography picture — when the whole crew is moving, you want to see the whole crew. Category D, E, and F are progressively further back. B and C are the sweet spot: close enough to feel the energy of the performance, far enough back to take in the full formation work that makes Jabbawockeez visually stunning.
What time does Jabbawockeez start, and how long is the show?
Jabbawockeez runs two shows per night: 5:30 PM and 8:00 PM. The show runs Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday — dark on Tuesdays. The performance is 75 minutes with no intermission. Plan to arrive 20 minutes before showtime.
Can I take photos or video during Jabbawockeez?
No photography or video recording is permitted during the performance. This is standard for professional Las Vegas productions. Spike's advice: the Jabbawockeez show is built on precisely timed choreography and visual storytelling — watching through a phone screen means missing the actual experience. Put it away for 75 minutes.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for Las Vegas live entertainment. Jabbawockeez has been running at MGM Grand for years with a professional production track record — outright cancellations are rare. In the event of a production cancellation, refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
