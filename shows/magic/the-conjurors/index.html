What is the show?

What is The Conjurors: Secret Wonders?

The Conjurors: Secret Wonders is a 75-minute mentalism and mind-reading show at The Magicians Room on the LINQ Promenade starring Matthew Pomeroy and Natasha Lamb. Stories, predictions and psychological mystery give the show a theatrical feel, with the duo carrying the experience together.

The smaller showroom suits the material. Mind-reading pieces and darker mystery elements stay close to the audience, while Pomeroy and Lamb’s two-person format gives the show its own rhythm and personality.

The 30-second answer

Is The Conjurors: Secret Wonders worth seeing?

Yes if you like mentalism with a theatrical edge — mind reading, predictions and mystery presented by a two-person act in a small room.

The Conjurors leans into atmosphere as much as the individual effects. Matthew Pomeroy and Natasha Lamb use stories and mystery to connect the pieces, and The Magicians Room keeps the whole thing intimate.

Frequently asked questions

Where is The Magicians Room and how do I find it?
The Magicians Room is at the LINQ Promenade — 3545 S Las Vegas Blvd, Suite L-07B, Las Vegas, NV 89109. Important: the entrance is off the LINQ Promenade, the open-air shopping and dining strip, not through the LINQ Hotel casino. Head to the Promenade and follow signage to Suite L-07B. Plan to arrive at least 15 minutes early — reserved seating assignments are subject to change for guests arriving 15 minutes or more after the show begins.
Is The Conjurors appropriate for kids?
Yes — Secret Wonders is designed as a warm, interactive family experience, with a recommended guest age of 5 and up. It's built around audience choices and childlike wonder rather than adult content, so it plays well for families. Every guest, regardless of age, needs a ticket.
Which seats are best — what's the difference between the sections?
The Magicians Room is a cozy showroom, so all three sections stay relatively close to the stage. Section 1 is closest, Section 2 sits through the middle, and Section 3 gives you a little more distance while still staying inside the compact room.
When should I arrive?
Plan to arrive at the LINQ Promenade at least 15 to 30 minutes before the 6:30 PM showtime. Reserved seating assignments are subject to change for guests who arrive 15 minutes or more after the show begins, so give yourself time to find Suite L-07B on the Promenade and get settled before it starts.
Can I take photos?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Note that reserved seating assignments are subject to change for guests arriving 15 minutes or more after the show begins. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
