What is the show?

What is Paranormal?

Paranormal is a 75-minute Las Vegas show starring mentalist Frederic Da Silva at the Laugh Factory inside Horseshoe. It combines mind reading, hypnosis and magic in a compact production that focuses more on psychological mystery than on elaborate scenery.

The show’s appeal is the succession of impossible-looking demonstrations involving thoughts, predictions and people in the room. If you prefer mentalism and hypnosis to giant illusion boxes or effects-heavy staging, Paranormal stays much closer to that style.

The 30-second answer

Is Paranormal worth seeing?

Yes if you want a compact mentalism show where hypnosis and mind reading matter more than elaborate sets or large-scale stage illusions.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Paranormal performed in Las Vegas?
Paranormal plays the Laugh Factory inside Horseshoe Las Vegas (formerly Bally's), at 3645 S Las Vegas Blvd. Follow signage from the casino floor — if you remember the resort by its old name, give yourself a few extra minutes to find your way. Plan to arrive at least 30 minutes before showtime.
Is Paranormal a comedy show, since it's at the Laugh Factory?
No — Paranormal is a magic, hypnosis, and mind-reading show, not stand-up comedy. It's staged inside the Laugh Factory showroom at Horseshoe Las Vegas, which hosts more than one kind of show; Frederic Da Silva's performance is mentalism, not a comedy set.
Is Paranormal appropriate for kids?
Yes, with a minimum age — guests must be at least 5 years old, and everyone needs a ticket regardless of age. Guests 18 and under must be accompanied by an adult. It's one of the more family-friendly mentalism shows on the Strip, though the hypnosis segments do involve adult volunteers pulled from the crowd.
What's the Sweet Spot for Paranormal seating?
Premium is the Sweet Spot — the row directly behind VIP. The Laugh Factory at Horseshoe is a cozy showroom to begin with, so Premium gets you close enough for the mind-reading to feel personal without paying the VIP price. General Admission, toward the back and sides, is still a solid view in a room this size.
When should I arrive?
Plan to arrive at Horseshoe Las Vegas at least 30 minutes before your 4:00 PM showtime. Give yourself extra time to find the Laugh Factory showroom, especially if you're not familiar with the resort's newer Horseshoe branding.
Can I bring my phone or camera?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation, you'll be notified and given refund options. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
