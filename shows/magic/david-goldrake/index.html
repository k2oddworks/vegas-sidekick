About David Goldrake

Step into the shadows of the LINQ Promenade, where the International Man of Mystery commands the intimate sanctuary of The Magician's Room.

Hailing from Luxembourg and fluent in seven languages, David Goldrake is a world-class illusionist who has performed for royalty, Fortune 500 companies, and audiences around the globe. Leaving behind the sprawling caverns of his past casino residencies, he now weaves his enigmatic spells mere inches from your eyes. Within this darkened, adults-only theater, ancient showmanship collides with modern sorcery — unspoken thoughts are plucked from thin air, gravity surrenders to impossible levitations, and the breathless peril of a Houdini-style water escape unfolds with heart-stopping proximity.
