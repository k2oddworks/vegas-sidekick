Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Where is The Magician's Room and how do I find it?
The Magician's Room is at the LINQ Promenade — 3545 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109. Important: the entrance is off the LINQ Promenade, the open-air shopping and dining strip, not through the LINQ Hotel casino. Head to the Promenade and follow signage to The Magician's Room. Plan to arrive at least 15 minutes early — reserved seating assignments are subject to change for guests arriving 15 minutes or more after the show begins.
Is there an age restriction for David Goldrake?
Yes — David Goldrake is an adults-only show. All guests must be 18 years of age or older. It's an intimate, sophisticated evening of mystery and illusion in a darkened theater, not a family magic show.
Which seats are best — what's the difference between the sections?
The Magician's Room is a cozy, intimate theater, so honestly there isn't a bad seat — every section is close to the stage. Section 2 is the Sweet Spot: the centered, straight-on view that takes in the whole show. Section 1 is closest to the stage and best for fans who want to see the sleight of hand and close-up magic right up under their nose. Section 3 is still close and gives you the full picture from a step back.
When should I arrive?
Plan to arrive at the LINQ Promenade at least 15 to 30 minutes before the 8:30 PM showtime. Reserved seating assignments are subject to change for guests who arrive 15 minutes or more after the show begins, so give yourself time to find The Magician's Room on the Promenade, grab a drink, and get settled before it starts.
Can I take photos?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography. In an intimate room like this, staying present and off your phone is a big part of the experience — a lot of the magic happens just inches from your seat.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Note that reserved seating assignments are subject to change for guests arriving 15 minutes or more after the show begins. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
