What is the show?

What is Piff The Magic Dragon?

Piff The Magic Dragon is a 75-minute comedy-magic show at the Flamingo starring Piff, a deadpan magician in a green dragon costume, alongside Mr. Piffles, his performing chihuahua. The act mixes real magic with a deliberately dry, absurd comic persona.

That contrast is the whole identity of the show. The tricks are serious enough to stand on their own, but Piff undercuts the usual dramatic magician presentation with sarcasm, awkwardness and the running visual joke of being a dragon on a Vegas stage.

The 30-second answer

Is Piff The Magic Dragon worth seeing?

Yes if deadpan comedy is as important to you as the magic; Piff’s dragon persona and Mr. Piffles make this a very different night from a traditional illusion show.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the Flamingo Showroom?
The Flamingo Showroom is inside Flamingo Las Vegas, on the Strip at 3555 S Las Vegas Blvd. Follow the showroom signage from the main casino floor — it's a short, well-marked walk from the lobby. Plan to arrive about 30–45 minutes before showtime to find parking, get through the casino floor, and get seated before the lights go down.
Is Piff The Magic Dragon appropriate for kids?
Yes, with one caveat: the show is billed for guests 8 and older, and anyone 18 or under must be accompanied by an adult. The magic and the magic-performing chihuahua play great for kids, but Piff's stage persona is dry, sarcastic, and occasionally sprinkled with adult-leaning one-liners — think a comedy club sense of humor wrapped around a genuinely great magic show. Most families with older kids and teens have a great time; it's not a show for toddlers.
What's the difference between the seating sections?
The pink and purple seating are the Vegas Sidekick Sweet Spot / Our Pick. Orange is nearest the stage, pink and purple fill the middle of the main floor, cyan sits farther back around the MIX area, and the balcony is the separate section on the right side of the chart.
When should I arrive?
Doors typically open about 30 minutes before showtime. Plan to arrive at Flamingo Las Vegas 45 minutes early — that covers parking or rideshare drop-off, the walk through the casino to the showroom, and getting settled before the 7:00 PM start. Piff opens strong, and latecomers get noticed in a room this size.
Can I take photos during the show?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Piff The Magic Dragon runs a consistent weekly schedule at the Flamingo Showroom, and outright cancellations are rare. Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation, you'll be notified with refund options provided.
