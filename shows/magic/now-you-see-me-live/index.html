Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Where is Now You See Me LIVE performed?
Now You See Me LIVE is staged at the MGM Grand Theater inside the MGM Grand Hotel & Casino, 3799 S Las Vegas Blvd, at the south end of the Strip. Enter through the main MGM Grand casino and follow signage to the theater. Plan to arrive at the resort 45 minutes before showtime — the MGM Grand is one of the largest hotels in the world, and it takes longer to get from the parking garage or rideshare drop-off to the theater than most guests expect.
Is Now You See Me LIVE appropriate for kids?
Guests must be 5 years of age or older to attend, and anyone under 18 must be accompanied by an adult. The show is a family-friendly magic spectacular — illusions, escapes, and audience interaction with no adult content. Kids who love magic, mystery, and big stage spectacle will be captivated. The roughly two-hour runtime is worth keeping in mind for younger children.
Which seats are best for Now You See Me LIVE?
The Premium Center section is the Sweet Spot. Magic rewards a clean, straight-on sightline — you want to see the hands, the props, and the reveals without an angle working against you. Premium Center puts you dead-center facing the stage where the illusions read exactly as they're designed to. General seats behind it still offer a strong full-stage view, and the Side section is the budget-friendly angled option.
What are the showtimes?
Now You See Me LIVE offers three showtimes — 4:00 PM, 7:00 PM, and 9:30 PM — but the days and times performed vary week to week. Always confirm the exact date and time at checkout before you book. The earlier 4:00 PM slot is a great option for families with younger kids or anyone building a dinner-and-show evening.
How long is the show?
Now You See Me LIVE runs approximately two hours. It's a full-length production with a beginning, middle, and payoff — not a quick club-length set. Plan your evening around the roughly two-hour runtime plus arrival and exit time at the MGM Grand.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a production cancellation, you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
