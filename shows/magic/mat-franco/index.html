What is the show?

What is Mat Franco?

Mat Franco: Magic Reinvented Nightly is a 90-minute magic show at The LINQ built around modern sleight of hand, illusion and Franco’s easygoing personality. The production is polished, but it keeps the performer at the center instead of burying the magic under giant scenery or nonstop effects.

Franco was the first magician to win America’s Got Talent, and the Vegas show expands that personality-first style to a full theater. Expect a contemporary magic show that feels more conversational and performer-driven than a dark, effects-heavy illusion spectacle.

The 30-second answer

Is Mat Franco worth seeing?

Yes if you want modern magic centered on Mat Franco’s personality rather than a giant effects-heavy illusion spectacle.

He was the first magician to win America’s Got Talent, and the Vegas production gives that personality-first style a full theater without burying it under spectacle. At 90 minutes, it’s a straightforward evening magic pick in the middle of the Strip.
