What is the show?

What is An R-Rated Magic Show?

An R-Rated Magic Show is a 65-minute Las Vegas magic-and-comedy production starring Grant Freeman at V Theater inside Miracle Mile Shops. The point is not family magic with a few adult jokes added later—the comedy is deliberately raunchy and the show is built for an older crowd.

Magic and stand-up share the format, so the night moves between illusions and uncensored comedy instead of treating the tricks with a serious, mysterious tone. It is the adult alternative for someone who likes magic but wants the room and material to feel closer to a late-night comedy show.

Frequently asked questions

Is An R-Rated Magic Show appropriate for kids or families?
The current listing recommends the show for ages 18 and older, but does not enforce an age restriction. The material is R-rated, with raunchy and sexual humor, so families should use that content guidance when deciding whether it fits their group.
What's the difference between VIP, Reserved, and Rear seating?
VIP is the closest rows to the stage — best for catching every detail, but also where Grant Freeman is most likely to ask for volunteers (it's always a request, never a requirement — you can always say no). Reserved is the sweet spot: close enough to catch everything, far enough back to lower your odds of being asked. Rear is budget seating at the back of the V Theater — you'll still see and hear the whole show, just from farther away.
Where is the V Theater and how do I find it?
The V Theater is inside Miracle Mile Shops, the indoor mall that runs through Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino — it's not accessible directly from the hotel lobby. Enter through the Miracle Mile Shops mall entrance and follow signage inside to the V Theater. Give yourself extra time on a first visit; the mall is larger than it looks and there are multiple showrooms in the same complex.
Can I bring a camera or record the show?
No. Cameras and audio/video recording are not permitted during the performance.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the rare event of a show cancellation, you'll be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
