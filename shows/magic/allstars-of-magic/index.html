Frequently asked questions

Who are the magicians performing?
The lineup rotates — Allstars of Magic features multiple world-class performers whose specific acts change regularly. Check the current bill before purchasing to see who's on stage for your date. The multi-performer format means the show always features a range of magic styles: the roster changes but the commitment to world-class acts does not.
Is this the upstairs V Theater or the main floor?
Allstars of Magic performs at the main floor V Theater — not the upstairs V2 showroom. The V Theater inside Miracle Mile Shops runs two different performance spaces. The upstairs V2 hosts The Mentalist. Allstars of Magic is on the main floor. When you navigate inside Miracle Mile Shops, stay on the ground level and follow signs to the V Theater rather than taking stairs or escalators up.
Is this show good for kids?
Yes — Allstars of Magic is one of the most family-friendly magic shows on the Strip. All ages welcome, no restrictions. Children 2 and under sit on a lap at no charge. The multi-performer format and 60-minute runtime work particularly well for younger audiences: no single act overstays its welcome and the variety keeps attention through the full hour. The 4 PM time slot also works well for families who want an afternoon activity before dinner.
How do I find the V Theater inside Miracle Mile Shops?
Enter through Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino at 3663 S Las Vegas Blvd and follow signs through Miracle Mile Shops to the V Theater. Stay on the main floor — Allstars of Magic is at the main V Theater, not the upstairs V2 space. Miracle Mile Shops can be disorienting to navigate. Seating opens 30 minutes before showtime — plan to be at the hotel 45 minutes early so you have time to find the theater, use the restroom, and grab a drink before you sit down.
Why does this show only run three days a week?
Allstars of Magic runs Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday at 4 PM. The V Theater hosts multiple productions — this schedule is built around the venue's programming across its multiple shows. Three performance days per week is consistent and gives you flexibility to plan around. Always verify the current schedule before purchasing.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Refunds are available for exceptional circumstances such as the unlikely event that a show cancels. Check the full terms and conditions before purchasing.
