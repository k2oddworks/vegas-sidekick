Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Is this show good for kids?
Yes — Nathan Burton Comedy Magic is one of the best family magic shows on the Strip. All ages welcome, no age restrictions, and children 2 and under sit on a lap for free. The comedy and magic combination works on every age in the room simultaneously, and Nathan stays after the show to meet the audience and take photos. For families with young children, that post-show meet-and-greet tends to be a highlight.
Can I take photos during the show?
Still photos may be welcome as long as they don't interfere with the show or other guests — check with your usher before shooting. Flash photography is strictly prohibited. Video recording is not permitted. If an usher asks you to stop, put the phone away. Nathan does meet the audience after the show, so there will be a clear opportunity for photos then.
Where exactly is the Nathan Burton Theater?
The Nathan Burton Theater is located inside FlyOver Las Vegas at 3771 S Las Vegas Blvd, Suite 160 — between Hard Rock Cafe and Ross Dress for Less on the Strip. Enter through the FlyOver Las Vegas entrance on the Strip; the theater is just inside. Note that the show no longer performs at Planet Hollywood — some older listings may still show that address. Paid self-parking is available in the garage off Las Vegas Boulevard.
Does Nathan Burton perform every day?
The show performs daily at 4:00 PM. Always verify the current schedule before purchasing.
How important is it to arrive early?
Arriving at least 30 minutes before showtime is recommended. Your seat may be given away to walk-up guests if you haven't arrived by showtime — this is standard policy at the Nathan Burton Theater. The FlyOver building is easy to find on the Strip, so give yourself enough time to park or get dropped off and get settled in your section before the show starts.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Refunds are available for exceptional circumstances such as the unlikely event that a show cancels. Check the full terms and conditions before purchasing.
