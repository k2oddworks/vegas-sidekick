About MIND2MIND

MIND2MIND turns the audience into part of the mystery — by the end of 75 minutes, you'll wonder if you just witnessed magic, telepathy, or a secret human superpower.

James Harrington and Marina Liani built their reputation as world-champion mentalists on international television — appearances on America's Got Talent and Britain's Got Talent, along with praise from Simon Cowell, Criss Angel, and David Blaine. MIND2MIND is their Las Vegas residency: a thrilling, psychological roller coaster that blends intuition, illusion, and predictions that shouldn't be possible, pulling audience members directly into the act.

Azul Lounge is a small room — there isn't a bad seat, and being close is part of what makes an act like this land. What to Expect

🔮 World-Champion Mentalism
James Harrington and Marina Liani have carried their reputation across international television — America's Got Talent, Britain's Got Talent, and praise from Simon Cowell, Criss Angel, and David Blaine. MIND2MIND is the Las Vegas version of that act, built for a room small enough to make the mystery feel personal.
🎭 An Interactive, Audience-Driven Show
This isn't a watch-from-a-distance illusion show. James and Marina pull audience members directly into the act — intuition, illusion, and predictions that shouldn't be possible, built around real reactions from real people in the room.
🏛️ An Intimate Lounge, Not a Big Theater
Azul Lounge is small by Strip standards, and that's the point — mentalism depends on proximity. There isn't a "best" seat here the way there is in a 1,500-seat theater; every seat is close enough for the act to land.
📅 Five Nights a Week
Monday, Tuesday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, with two shows Friday and Saturday night. Dark Wednesday and Thursday. The schedule leaves easy room to build a night around it.
