What is the show?

What is MIND2MIND?

MIND2MIND is a 75-minute mentalism show at Azul Lounge inside Fontainebleau Las Vegas starring James Harrington and Marina Liani. The act is built around predictions, mind reading and psychological-style mystery rather than giant stage illusions.

With two mentalists sharing the show, the presentation can move between individual feats, predictions and apparent mind reading. The appeal is the mystery of how the information is revealed rather than a fixed sequence of visual tricks.

The 30-second answer

Is MIND2MIND worth seeing?

Yes if you would rather watch predictions and mind-reading than big-box illusions, with two mentalists carrying the show.

James Harrington and Marina Liani keep the focus on predictions, mind reading and psychological-style mystery. If you're bringing someone who's never seen a live mentalism act up close, this is a strong pick.

MIND2MIND is a Las Vegas residency from James Harrington and Marina Liani, two world-champion mentalists who've appeared on both America's Got Talent and Britain's Got Talent — with praise from Simon Cowell, Criss Angel, and David Blaine along the way. It's a 75-minute, 21-and-over show inside the intimate Azul Lounge at Fontainebleau Las Vegas, built around pulling the audience directly into the act. This isn't a big-stage illusion show — it's close, interactive, and genuinely hard to explain afterward.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Azul Lounge inside Fontainebleau Las Vegas?
Azul Lounge is inside Fontainebleau Las Vegas, on the Las Vegas Strip. Follow signage from the casino floor to Azul Lounge — as with any new resort, give yourself extra time on your first visit, since the building is large and the lounge isn't right off the main entrance. Plan to arrive at least 30–45 minutes before showtime.
Is MIND2MIND appropriate for kids?
No. MIND2MIND is a 21-and-over show — guests must be 21 years of age or older, and a valid photo ID is required at the door. This isn't a family show; it's built for an adult audience in an intimate lounge setting.
What's the difference between Theater Seating and the Cabaret Tables?
Theater Seating (sections 101 and 102) is closest to the stage. The Cabaret Tables are the row directly behind, between the seating sections and the bar. The main difference is style: Theater Seating is individual reserved chairs, while the Cabaret Tables are shared round tables, better suited to groups who want to sit together — Azul Lounge is small enough that neither is a meaningfully worse view. Section 102 also includes wheelchair-accessible seating.
When should I arrive?
Plan to arrive at Fontainebleau Las Vegas at least 30–45 minutes before showtime. Azul Lounge is an intimate room, so seating fills in quickly, and you'll want time to find the lounge, get seated, and settle in before James Harrington and Marina Liani take the stage.
Can I bring my phone or camera?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation, you'll be notified and given refund options. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
