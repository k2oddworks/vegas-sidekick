What is the show?

What is MIND2MIND?

MIND2MIND is a 75-minute mentalism show at Azul Lounge inside Fontainebleau Las Vegas starring James Harrington and Marina Liani. The show centers on predictions, apparent mind reading and psychological mystery — the kind of reveals that make you wonder how they could possibly know what they know.

Having two mentalists at the center is the hook. It gives MIND2MIND a clear two-person identity, with predictions and apparent mind reading carrying the full 75 minutes. Azul Lounge keeps the experience intimate, so the focus stays on the performers, the mystery and the moment each reveal lands.

The 30-second answer

Is MIND2MIND worth seeing?

Yes if you like predictions, apparent mind reading and the kind of reveal that makes you immediately start working backward to figure out how it happened.

James Harrington and Marina Liani are the center of the show. MIND2MIND gives you a full evening of mentalism built around predictions, mind-reading effects and psychological mystery, with the intimate Azul Lounge keeping the attention on the performers and the reveals.

Harrington and Liani are world-champion mentalists who've appeared on both America's Got Talent and Britain's Got Talent. Their Las Vegas residency began in June 2026 and is currently extended through February 2, 2027. Performances run Friday through Tuesday at 8 PM, with doors at 7:30 PM. Guests ages 12–17 attend with a parent or guardian; guests 18 and older need valid photo ID.

Seat guide

Where should you sit at MIND2MIND?

Sections 101 and 102 face the stage, with four cabaret tables behind them. Section 101 is on the left as you face the stage; Section 102 is on the right. Tables 2 and 3 sit nearer the center, while Tables 1 and 4 sit toward the sides.

Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Where is Azul Lounge inside Fontainebleau Las Vegas?
Azul Lounge is inside Fontainebleau Las Vegas, on the Las Vegas Strip. Follow signage from the casino floor to Azul Lounge — as with any new resort, give yourself extra time on your first visit, since the building is large and the lounge isn't right off the main entrance. Doors open at 7:30 PM for the 8 PM performance, so plan to be at Azul Lounge by then.
Is MIND2MIND appropriate for kids?
MIND2MIND admits guests ages 12–17 with a parent or guardian. Guests 18 and older may attend with valid photo ID.
What's the difference between Theater Seating and the Cabaret Tables?
Theater Seating (sections 101 and 102) is closest to the stage. The Cabaret Tables are the row directly behind, between the seating sections and the bar. The main difference is style: Theater Seating is individual reserved chairs, while the Cabaret Tables are shared round tables, better suited to groups who want to sit together — Azul Lounge is small enough that neither is a meaningfully worse view. Section 102 also includes wheelchair-accessible seating.
When should I arrive?
Plan to arrive at Fontainebleau Las Vegas at least 30–45 minutes before showtime. Azul Lounge is an intimate room, so seating fills in quickly, and you'll want time to find the lounge, get seated, and settle in before James Harrington and Marina Liani take the stage.
Can I bring my phone or camera?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation, you'll be notified and given refund options. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
