Frequently asked questions

What kind of magic does Shin Lim perform?
Close-up card magic — specifically sleight of hand and card manipulation performed at a speed and precision that holds multiple Guinness World Records. This is not the same format as a stage illusion show with large apparatus, assistants, and set pieces. It's a man with a deck of cards doing things that should be impossible, close enough that you can watch carefully and still not figure it out. LIMITLESS is 90 minutes of that.
What section should I choose?
Section 2 is the Sweet Spot — dead center of the main floor arc. For a close-up magic show, the center sight line is essential. VIP puts you closest to the stage and is the right call if maximum proximity matters to you. Sections 1 and 3 flank Section 2 on the main floor and are strong second choices. For this particular show — where the magic happens at a human scale rather than across a large stage — center positioning in the main floor arc is where the performance lands best.
Can I bring a camera or backpack?
No cameras, backpacks, large bags, iPads, or laptops are permitted into the Palazzo Theatre. Travel light — phone in your pocket, essentials only. Cameras are specifically banned, and flash photography and video recording are prohibited during the show. Leave camera equipment at the hotel.
Is LIMITLESS appropriate for kids?
Children ages 5 and older are welcome. Everyone must have a ticket to enter — no lap sitting. The show has no adult content; it's close-up magic. The only caveat is that the performance is quiet and precise, which works better for kids who can focus and pay attention for 90 minutes than for very young children accustomed to louder, more visually stimulating entertainment.
Where is the Palazzo Theatre inside The Venetian Resort?
The Palazzo Theatre is located inside The Venetian Resort Las Vegas at 3325 S Las Vegas Blvd. The Venetian and The Palazzo share the same complex on the north-central Strip. Follow signs to The Palazzo Theatre once you're inside the property. The resort is large — give yourself at least 20 minutes from parking or rideshare drop-off to reach the theater and get seated before showtime.
