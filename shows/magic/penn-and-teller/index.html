The 30-second answer

Is Penn & Teller worth seeing?

Penn & Teller pair large-stage magic with comedy, skepticism and the personalities that have defined their act for decades.

This is not a generic illusion show with interchangeable performers. Penn does most of the talking, Teller works silently, and the fun is in watching them play with how magic is presented while still delivering the trick.

🌵
Kris’s take

Penn & Teller is the pick when you want headliner magic with a point of view. The comedy and skepticism matter as much as the tricks, which is exactly why the show still feels distinct after such a long Vegas run.

Good fit

A strong fit if you want a 90-minute headliner magic show where comedy and the duo’s personalities matter as much as the illusions.

Before you go

FAQ

Is there a Penn & Teller meet-and-greet after the show?
Rio currently offers a separate post-show Penn & Teller Meet & Greet Experience. A separate show ticket is required, so do not assume a standard show ticket includes the meet-and-greet.
Why doesn’t Teller talk?
Teller performs silently as part of the stage character he developed early in his career. Penn handles most of the spoken material, and that contrast is central to the duo’s performance style.
Is Penn & Teller appropriate for children?
Guests must be 5 years of age or older.
Can I take photos during Penn & Teller?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren’t distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography.
Where is the Penn & Teller Theater?
Penn & Teller perform at the Penn & Teller Theater inside Rio Las Vegas at 3700 W Flamingo Rd, just west of the Strip.
How long is Penn & Teller?
The current listed runtime is 90 minutes.
When does Penn & Teller perform?
Current listings agree on Thursday through Sunday performances, but current sources disagree on the fixed start hour. Check the live calendar for the exact time on your date.
How much are Penn & Teller tickets?
Tickets currently start at $66. Other price points may be available depending on the performance and seating section.
