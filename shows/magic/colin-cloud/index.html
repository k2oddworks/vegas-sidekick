What is the show?

What is Colin Cloud: Mastermind?

Colin Cloud: Mastermind is a 90-minute mentalism show at Harrah’s Cabaret built around deduction, mind reading and psychological-style mystery rather than large stage illusions. Cloud’s “real-life Sherlock Holmes” persona gives the show a detective-like frame for the mental feats.

The appeal is watching him appear to pull information from people and situations rather than watching boxes, levitation rigs or giant scenic effects. If your idea of magic leans more toward impossible predictions and reading people than traditional illusion, this is firmly in that lane.

Yes if mind reading and deduction interest you more than big stage illusions; Colin Cloud keeps the focus on mentalism and psychological-style mystery.

Frequently asked questions

What is Colin Cloud known for?
Colin Cloud is a Scottish forensic scientist and mentalist known as The Real-Life Sherlock Holmes. He became a sensation on Britain's Got Talent, where his ability to deduce facts about complete strangers — their professions, relationships, psychology — left both audiences and celebrity judges unable to explain what they'd witnessed. He has since performed for heads of state and royal families internationally before bringing Mastermind to Las Vegas. His mentalism is sharper and faster than the traditional stage format, closer to live detective work than a magic show.
Should I sit closer to the stage if I want to be in the show?
P1 (front-center) gives you the highest chance of being directly involved in a routine. That said, Colin Cloud pulls volunteers from all sections throughout the show — sitting in P2 or P3 doesn't guarantee you won't be called on. The mentalism reaches the full room. If participation is specifically what you want, P1 is the strategic call. If you just want to watch the best deduction performance in Las Vegas right now, every seat delivers that.
How is this different from The Mentalist at Planet Hollywood?
Both are mentalism shows but they operate very differently. Gerry McCambridge at The Mentalist builds atmospherically and works the room in a warmer, more theatrical style. Colin Cloud is faster, more confrontational, and leans harder into the deduction mechanism — the Sherlock Holmes comparison isn't marketing; it's genuinely the operational mode of the show. McCambridge is a classic mentalist; Cloud performs more like an interrogator. Different shows for different preferences in the same category.
Is the show appropriate for younger guests?
Ages 10 and older are welcome. Everyone requires a ticket regardless of age — no exceptions. The show contains psychological content and some confrontational moments that work best for guests 10 and up. No explicit content, no strong language. The age floor is appropriate — younger children may not engage with the psychological dimension of the mentalism the way older audiences do.
Where is Harrah's Las Vegas on the Strip?
Harrah's Las Vegas is located at 3475 S Las Vegas Blvd, centrally positioned on the Strip between The LINQ Hotel and Caesars Palace. It's one of the most walkable Strip properties — close to the High Roller, Flamingo, and Bally's. Easy rideshare access and on-site self-parking. Harrah's Cabaret is inside the hotel; follow signage from the main entrance.
