What is X Rocks?

X Rocks is a 70-minute adults-only topless revue at Horseshoe Las Vegas built around classic rock and heavy metal. The production combines choreographed dance, familiar guitar-driven songs, stage sets and a comedic interlude instead of using a pop or burlesque-standard soundtrack.

The music changes the personality of the whole revue. Lighting, costumes and choreography are shaped around rock-concert energy, making X Rocks the most obvious fit for visitors who want an adult production with louder, harder-edged musical taste.

Frequently asked questions

What makes X Rocks different from X Burlesque and other topless revues on the Strip?
X Rocks comes from the same husband-and-wife producing team behind X Burlesque, Matt and Angela Stabile — but it's a separate, standalone production, not a spin-off or a re-skin. Where X Burlesque runs as a variety-style topless revue, X Rocks is built entirely around iconic rock and heavy metal songs, reimagined through sultry, high-energy choreography and elaborate sets. Producer Angela Stabile has described it as pairing powerhouse rock anthems with erotic, high-energy choreography and eye-catching sets, with surprises built into nearly every number — think of X Rocks as the louder, riff-driven cousin to X Burlesque's classic revue format.
What is the age restriction for X Rocks?
Guests must be 18 years of age or older. Bring a valid photo ID — it may be requested at the door.
What are the seating zones at the X Rocks Theatre?
There are four zones. Rockstar sits front and center, split into two blocks flanking the center aisle, closest to the stage. VIP is the middle band, spanning the center section and both side wings — this is the show's Sweet Spot. General Admission is the outer and back band, also spanning the center and both wings, and is the farthest of the three main seating areas. Icon is a small, standalone elevated box at the very back and center of the room, separate from the other three.
When should I arrive, and can I take photos during the show?
Seating at the X Rocks Theatre inside Horseshoe Las Vegas begins about 30 minutes before the 9:30 PM curtain, but plan to arrive at the property 45 minutes early. That gives you time to find the theater, use the restroom, and grab a drink before you sit down. Once the show starts, cameras and audio/video recording devices are not allowed.
Why is VIP the Sweet Spot instead of the front-row Rockstar section?
Rockstar puts you right against the stage, which some guests love, but at that range the big-picture elements — the sets, the lighting design, the full shape of the choreography — are harder to appreciate. VIP pulls back just enough to take in the whole number while still feeling close and loud. It's the seat that balances intimacy with the visuals the production is built around.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
X Rocks runs Monday, Thursday and Saturday with a consistent schedule, so outright cancellations are uncommon. Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the rare case of a cancellation, you'll be notified and given refund options.
