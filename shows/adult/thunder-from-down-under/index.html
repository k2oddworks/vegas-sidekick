What is the show?

What is Thunder From Down Under?

Thunder From Down Under is a 70-minute adults-only male revue at Thunderland Theater inside Excalibur. The show is built around choreographed dance, costumes, strip-tease performance and the high-energy personality of its Australian cast.

It is designed as a group-night-out show rather than a traditional theatrical story, which is why it is especially common for birthdays and bachelorette parties. The appeal is the combination of dance, stage charisma and a party atmosphere inside a full Vegas showroom.

The 30-second answer

Is Thunder From Down Under worth seeing?

Yes if you want a high-energy male revue built around dancing, costumes and crowd interaction, especially for a group celebration.

Thunder From Down Under is the hottest male revue show in Las Vegas — bringing sexy men straight from Australia to the Thunderland Theater inside Excalibur Hotel. These Aussie imports have built a legendary reputation for delivering one of the most entertaining and interactive nights out on the Strip.

Whether it's a bachelorette party, a divorce celebration, a birthday, or just a girls night out, Thunder From Down Under delivers. Expect 70 minutes of dancing, strip tease, elaborate costumes — firemen, military outfits, cowboys, and more — plus a cast of chiseled performers who will find their way into the crowd and onto the showroom tables.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Thunder From Down Under located?
Thunder From Down Under performs in the Thunderland Theater inside Excalibur Hotel &amp; Casino at 3850 S Las Vegas Blvd — midway down the Strip. The theater has its own dedicated entrance. Plan to arrive 20–30 minutes early to clear entry, get seated, and settle in before the 8 PM showtime.
What are the age requirements for Thunder From Down Under?
All guests must be at least 18 years old to attend. A valid government-issued photo ID is required at entry. No exceptions — children and minors are not admitted under any circumstances.
Can I take photos or video during Thunder From Down Under?
Flash photography and video recording are not permitted during the show. Leave the camera in your bag and be present — this is the kind of show where being in the moment beats any photo you'd take.
Why does Spike recommend Category A and Category B?
Category A puts you close to the stage for stronger performer detail. Category B keeps good sight lines and proximity while giving you a slightly wider view of the full staging.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for Las Vegas live entertainment. Thunder From Down Under runs nightly and rarely cancels. In the event of a production cancellation, refund options will be provided. Review the full terms at checkout before purchasing.
