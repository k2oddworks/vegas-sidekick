What is the show?

What is Atomic Saloon Show?

Atomic Saloon Show is a 75-minute adults-only Spiegelworld production at The Venetian that mixes circus, comedy and variety inside a deliberately rowdy Wild West saloon setting. The theme gives the show a loose framework, but the real engine is the succession of physical acts and adult comedy.

Expect acrobatics and specialty performance presented with the same irreverent tone as the comedy. The saloon atmosphere, close staging and bawdy humor are part of the identity, giving the circus acts a rowdy, up-close energy.

Yes if you want an adults-only Vegas show where circus acts and dirty comedy share the same deliberately chaotic saloon.

Frequently asked questions

Is Atomic Saloon Show appropriate for everyone?
No — and it's proud of that. The show is strictly 18+ with bold, risqué adult humor and content not suitable for children. A valid government-issued ID is required at the door.
Where exactly is Atomic Saloon at The Venetian?
Atomic Saloon is in the Waterfall Atrium on Level 2M at The Venetian Las Vegas. Follow signs from the casino floor — the saloon's Wild West décor and marquee entrance make it unmissable.
Can I take photos during the show?
Photography is generally welcomed — it's part of the fun. Check with your usher when you arrive as policies can vary. Flash photography and full-act video recording may be restricted.
What's the difference between the 7:30 PM and 9:30 PM shows?
Both are the same 75-minute production. The 7:30 PM show works well if you have dinner plans after; the 9:30 PM late show often draws a slightly more energetic crowd. Both deliver the same experience.
Do I need to book in advance?
Yes — Atomic Saloon is a small venue and sells out regularly, especially Friday and Saturday nights. Book at least a few days in advance for weekend shows. Weeknight shows offer more flexibility.
Is there a dress code?
No formal dress code. Vegas casual or cocktail attire fits the saloon vibe. Some guests lean into the Wild West theme with cowboy hats and boots — it's welcomed. Just bring valid ID.
