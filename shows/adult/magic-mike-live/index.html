Frequently asked questions

Where is Magic Mike Live at SAHARA Las Vegas?
Magic Mike Live is performed in the MAGIC MIKE LIVE Theater inside SAHARA Las Vegas, located at 2535 S Las Vegas Blvd — at the north end of the Strip. The theater has its own dedicated entrance. Plan to arrive 20–30 minutes before showtime to allow time to get through entry, find your seats, and settle in before the show begins.
What are the age requirements for Magic Mike Live?
All guests must be at least 18 years old to attend Magic Mike Live. Guests must be 21 or older to consume alcohol. A valid government-issued photo ID is required at entry. No exceptions — children and minors are not permitted under any circumstances.
Can I take photos or video during Magic Mike Live?
Yes — cell phone photos and video recording are allowed during Magic Mike Live. Flash photography is not permitted. This is one of the few Vegas productions that explicitly allows photography, so feel free to capture the show.
What is the blue section, and why does Spike recommend it?
The blue sections (201–202) are the second ring from the stage — front and center in the main seating area. They offer the best combination of proximity and full-stage visibility. The floor sections (101–104) are closest but can be too close to take in the full choreographic picture. From the blue section you’re close enough to feel the energy and see detail, far enough back to appreciate the formations. That’s why Spike calls it the sweet spot for this show.
Is there a dress code for Magic Mike Live?
There’s no strict dress code, but this is a night-out show — most guests dress up for it. Bachelorette parties, girls nights, and birthday groups are the core audience. Smart casual or cocktail attire fits right in. Flip-flops and beachwear are out of place. Dress how you’d dress for a night out in Vegas — which is usually a good benchmark.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for Las Vegas live entertainment. Magic Mike Live has been running at SAHARA with a consistent schedule — outright cancellations are rare. In the event of a production cancellation, refund options will be provided. Review the full terms at checkout before purchasing.
