Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Is Rouge actually a topless show?
Yes. Rouge is a topless adult entertainment production show. Performers are topless throughout the performance. This is not a partially adult show or an "adult themes" show — it is a topless show. The 18+ age requirement exists for this reason and is enforced strictly at the door. If you're attending with a group, make sure everyone in the group understands what the show is before purchasing tickets.
What ID is accepted at the door?
Government-issued photo ID — driver's license, state ID card, or passport. The age minimum is 18 and is enforced with no exceptions. Bring valid ID for every person in your group. If anyone in your group cannot produce valid government-issued photo ID proving they are 18 or older, they will not be admitted to the theater.
What bags are allowed inside?
Small purses measuring 8"×8" or smaller are permitted inside the theater. Backpacks of any size are not allowed. If you arrive with a bag that doesn't meet the policy, the bell desk can hold it for you before the show — just build extra time into your arrival so you're not scrambling. The easiest approach is to leave non-essential bags at your hotel and bring only what fits in a small purse or your pockets.
What time does Rouge start?
Rouge runs nightly but showtimes vary by day of the week. Always check the current schedule on the booking page before purchasing — the time listed at checkout is your confirmed showtime. Seating opens 30 minutes before showtime — plan to arrive at The STRAT 30 minutes early so you have time for the ID check, bag check if needed, restrooms, and getting to your seat.
Can I take photos or video?
No. The use of cameras and audio/video devices are strictly prohibited during the show. This is enforced. Keep your device stowed once you take your seat. Smoking is also not permitted inside the theater.
Are refunds available?
Refunds are available for exceptional circumstances such as the unlikely event that a show cancels. Check the full terms and conditions before purchasing.
Where is The STRAT Hotel on the Strip?
The STRAT Hotel, Casino & SkyPod is located at 2000 S Las Vegas Blvd at the northern end of the Strip — north of Circus Circus, close to Sahara Avenue. It's easily accessible by rideshare from anywhere on the Strip and has on-site self-parking. The STRAT Theater is inside the hotel; follow signage from the main entrance.
