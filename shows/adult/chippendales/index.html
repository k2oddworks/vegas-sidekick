The 30-second answer

Is Chippendales worth seeing?

90-minute male revue with choreography, charm, and crowd interaction at the Chippendales Theatre inside Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino.

Chippendales is the original Las Vegas male revue — and after decades on the Strip, it still owns the room. At the Chippendales Theatre inside Planet Hollywood Resort, a cast of impossibly fit performers combines high-energy choreography, world-class lighting and production, and just the right amount of cheeky humor to create a night that stays with you.

This isn’t just a show — it’s a party. The men don’t stay on stage. They work the aisles, they interact with the crowd, and lucky guests get pulled onstage for moments they’ll be talking about for years. Whether you’re celebrating a bachelorette, a birthday, a divorce, or just a wild night out in Vegas, Chippendales delivers every time.

Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Can men attend Chippendales?
Yes — while Chippendales draws a predominantly female crowd, men are absolutely welcome and many enjoy it too. It’s a legitimately fun night out with a great sense of humor. Plenty of couples make it a date night.
Are cameras or phones allowed?
Photos and video recording are not permitted during the performance. Leave the lens in your bag and enjoy the experience in real time — you’ll thank yourself for it. Some venues allow photos during the lobby meet-and-greet after the show.
How close will I be to the performers?
Very close. The men come off the stage and into the audience throughout the show — every section gets attention. Some guests are even invited on stage. This is an intimate theater, so there’s no bad seat.
Is Chippendales good for a bachelorette party?
It’s the best bachelorette show in Las Vegas — full stop. The cast knows how to celebrate and spotlight the guest of honor. Let the box office know in advance that you’re celebrating; the performers will handle the rest in the most memorable way possible.
What should I wear?
Smart casual is the vibe. There’s no formal dress code. Many bachelorette groups go all out with themed outfits and matching accessories, which the performers always appreciate. Dress however makes you feel confident and ready for a great night.
Is there a meet-and-greet after the show?
The cast frequently mingles in the lobby after the show for photos and conversation. This isn’t formally guaranteed, but it’s a well-established Chippendales tradition. Arriving for the early show on Fri or Sat gives you more time afterward.
