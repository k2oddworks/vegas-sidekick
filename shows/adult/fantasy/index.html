What is the show?

What is Fantasy?

Fantasy is a 75-minute topless revue at the Atrium Showroom inside Luxor. The long-running production mixes choreographed dance, costumes, live vocals and comedy across a series of separate acts rather than relying on one repeated style of number.

That variety is what makes it a full revue instead of a simple dance set. Music and performance styles change through the show, with live singing and comedy breaking up the choreography. It is a classic Las Vegas adult-show format in a relatively compact runtime.

The 30-second answer

Is Fantasy worth seeing?

Yes if you want a classic Las Vegas topless revue that mixes choreographed dance with live vocals and comedy in one 75-minute show.

Fantasy has been part of Luxor’s nightlife lineup for more than 25 years. The show moves through 15 acts built around dance, costumes, music, live vocals and comedy, so it plays as a full revue rather than one repeated idea.

Seat guide

Where should you sit for Fantasy?

The Atrium Showroom is a compact curved room with Reserved 1 closest to the stage, Reserved 2 across the large middle block, then Reserved 3 and Reserved 4 behind it. Reserved 2 is the Sweet Spot / Our Pick for Fantasy.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fantasy appropriate for couples?

Yes. Fantasy is built as a polished 18+ Vegas revue with dance, live vocals and comedy, so it works naturally for couples who want an adult night at Luxor.

Is it really topless? What should I expect?

Yes. Fantasy is a topless revue with choreographed dance numbers, costumes, live vocals and comedy. All guests must be 18 or older, and valid photo ID is required.

Can men attend Fantasy?

Yes. Fantasy welcomes adult guests of all genders, including couples, groups and solo visitors.

Are cameras allowed during the show?

Cameras and audio/video recording devices are strictly prohibited during the performance.

Which seats should I get?

Reserved 2 is our pick. It covers the large middle block, rows D through J, and gives you a balanced view of the full stage. Reserved 1 is the closest option; Reserved 3 and 4 sit farther back in the same intimate room.

How long is Fantasy?

The current listed runtime is 75 minutes.
