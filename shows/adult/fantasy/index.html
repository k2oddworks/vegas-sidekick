The 30-second answer

Is Fantasy worth seeing?

75-minute adult revue — 15 energetic acts, stunning talent, and hilarious comedy — at the Atrium Theater inside Luxor Hotel & Casino.

Fantasy has ruled the Atrium Theater at Luxor for over 25 years — and it’s earned every one of them. Named “Best Adult Revue of All Time,” this topless revue brings 15 electrifying acts to the stage set to today’s hottest music, featuring a cast of stunningly talented dancers and a powerhouse live vocalist who ties it all together.

This isn’t just an adult show — it’s a full production. Sultry choreography, spectacular costumes, and seamless comedy keep the energy high between acts. Whether you’re on a couples’ date night, celebrating with a group, or flying solo looking for a great Vegas night out, Fantasy consistently delivers from the first act to the last bow.

Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Is Fantasy appropriate for couples?
Absolutely — Fantasy is one of Vegas’s most popular date-night shows. The combination of stunning dancers, live vocals, and sharp comedy makes it a genuinely enjoyable night out for both partners. Plenty of couples see Fantasy every year as part of their Vegas tradition.
Is it really topless? What should I expect?
Yes — Fantasy is a tasteful topless revue, which means some acts feature topless performances as part of the artistic presentation. The show is celebrated for doing this with class. It’s been called “The Strip’s Sexiest Tease” for 25+ years, so expectations are well set. 18+ only, and you must have valid photo ID.
Can men attend Fantasy?
Yes, all genders are welcome at Fantasy. The show draws a diverse crowd including couples, mixed groups, and solo visitors. The comedian and live vocalist give everyone something to love beyond just the dancers, making it an inclusive and fun night for all.
Are cameras allowed during the show?
No — cameras and audio/video recording devices are strictly not permitted during the performance. This rule is consistently enforced. Leave your camera in the bag and simply enjoy the show in real time. It makes the experience more immersive anyway.
Which seats should I get?
Reserved 2 is our pick — it’s the ideal zone in this intimate Atrium Theater. You get center sightlines across the full stage with enough distance to take everything in. That said, the Atrium Theater is genuinely cozy, so there’s not a bad seat in the house. If Reserved 2 is sold out, Reserved 1 puts you right at the front.
How long has Fantasy been running?
Over 25 years. Fantasy has been running continuously at Luxor since the late 1990s and has been awarded “Best Adult Revue of All Time” by Vegas industry reviewers. In a city where shows open and close every season, that longevity says everything about the quality of the production.
