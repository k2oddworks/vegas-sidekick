Frequently asked questions

What is The Empire Strips Back?
It's an adults-only burlesque parody built around iconic characters from a galaxy far, far away — jaw-dropping choreography, stunning costumes, and cheeky comedy, played entirely for laughs. Equal parts parody, cabaret, and theatrical production, not a tribute concert or a fan convention panel.
What is the age restriction — can a 17 year old attend?
No — guests must be 18 years of age or older. Valid ID may be requested at the door. This is an adults-only burlesque production, not appropriate for minors regardless of the playful sci-fi theme.
What's the difference between VIP Emperor, Premium, Preferred, and Standard seating?
VIP Emperor is the front row band closest to the stage. Premium and Preferred are the recommended Sweet Spot — the broad middle of the house, close enough to catch every detail of the choreography without paying front-row prices. Standard covers the side sections and the balcony farther back, a solid budget-friendly option in a room with good sightlines throughout.
Where do I enter the Empire Strips Back Theater and when should I arrive?
The Empire Strips Back Theater is inside the Rio Hotel. Follow signage from the casino floor to the theater entrance. Plan to arrive about 30 to 120 minutes early to park, find the theater, and get settled before the house lights go down.
Can I take photos or video during the show?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography or video recording during the performance.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
The Empire Strips Back runs nightly with consistent scheduling, so outright cancellations are rare. Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation, you'll be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms at checkout before purchasing.
