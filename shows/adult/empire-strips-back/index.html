About The Empire Strips Back

What happens when a galaxy far, far away meets a wildly entertaining burlesque show? This.

The Empire Strips Back takes the galaxy you know and love and gives it a hilarious, adults-only twist — a dazzling blend of burlesque, comedy, and incredible choreography. Fan-favorite characters get reimagined through stunning costumes, playful performances, and plenty of tongue-in-cheek humor, in a one-of-a-kind production packed with laughs, surprises, and eye-catching spectacle from beginning to end.

It's equal parts parody, cabaret, and theatrical production — pop culture celebrated with a fun, flirty edge and just the right amount of mischief. A must-see for couples, groups of friends, and anyone looking to experience something beyond the traditional Vegas show.

The Empire Strips Back Theater is a good-sized horseshoe room with solid sightlines throughout, but Premium and Preferred — the broad middle of the house — are the Sweet Spot here, close enough to catch every detail of the choreography without paying VIP Emperor prices.

Seating runs four zones: VIP Emperor up front, Premium and Preferred in the middle (the Sweet Spot), and Standard covering the side sections and balcony farther back. The show runs nightly — Monday through Thursday at 8:00 PM, Friday and Saturday at 7:00 and 9:30 PM, and Sunday at 7:00 PM.
