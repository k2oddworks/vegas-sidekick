What is X Country?

X Country is a 90-minute adults-only topless revue at Harrah’s Cabaret that builds its entire production around country music. Cowboy boots, country hits and choreographed dance give the show a honky-tonk identity instead of the pop-oriented soundtrack common to many adult revues.

The concept is simple but specific: take the Las Vegas topless revue format and run it through a country-music filter. That makes it an easy choice for groups who already like country and want an adult show that stays in that musical lane.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is X Country?
X Country is a topless adult revue set to country music, from the producers behind X Burlesque and X Rocks. It features high-energy choreographed dance acts performed by a talented cast, with stand-up comedy segments between acts to keep the energy varied and the laughs rolling. It’s the only country-themed topless revue in Las Vegas.
Is it appropriate for couples?
Absolutely — X Country is a great date-night show. The combination of music, performance, and comedy creates a full night of entertainment that both partners enjoy. It draws a mixed crowd including couples, bachelorette parties, and groups of friends looking for a memorable late-night Vegas experience.
Which seats should I get?
VIP and General Admission are our pick. VIP is split across the centered upper block and the two side sections around Front Porch. General Admission occupies the two upper corner sections. Front Porch is the large centered section closest to the stage.
Do I need to like country music to enjoy X Country?
Nope. Country music is the backdrop, but the show is really about energy, performance, and a great night out. Plenty of non-country fans have a blast — the format is designed to entertain everyone in the room, music taste notwithstanding.
Are cameras allowed?
No photos or video recording are permitted during the performance. This is strictly enforced. Leave the camera in your pocket, put your phone away, and enjoy the show live — it’s a better experience anyway.
How does X Country compare to X Burlesque?
Same production quality and creative team, different musical universe. X Burlesque leans into classic and contemporary pop/rock, while X Country is all Nashville — the energy and adult entertainment format are similar, but the vibe is distinctly Western. If you’ve loved X Burlesque, X Country is a natural next show.
