Is Zombie Burlesque worth seeing?

Campy zombie comedy, burlesque, specialty acts and live music at V Theater inside Miracle Mile Shops.

Adult comedy · Planet Hollywood Zombie Burlesque A campy 1950s zombie apocalypse turned into a comedy-burlesque variety show with a live band. More silly than scary, and very aware of how ridiculous the premise is.

Zombie Burlesque imagines a version of 1950s Las Vegas where the undead won and somehow opened a nightclub. The theme gives the show a reason to mix burlesque numbers, physical comedy, specialty acts and live music without pretending any of it needs to make normal sense.

🌵
Kris’s take

This is a comedy-first adult show with a deliberately silly theme. Think twice if you want a serious horror attraction or a traditional burlesque revue without the zombie-cabaret framing.

Frequently asked questions

Is Zombie Burlesque scary?
It is comedy first. The zombie theme is played like a campy 1950s B-movie rather than a haunted attraction, with burlesque, variety acts and a live band.
What is the age requirement for Zombie Burlesque?
Guests must be 16 or older. Valid identification may be requested at the venue.
When does Zombie Burlesque perform?
The regular schedule is Monday through Saturday at 8:00 PM, with Sunday dark. Confirm your date at checkout because schedules can change.
What is the difference between VIP and General Admission?
The VIP Quarantine Zone covers the closer rows, while General Admission is farther back. The closer section makes it easier to read the physical comedy, choreography and practical effects.

How long is Zombie Burlesque?
