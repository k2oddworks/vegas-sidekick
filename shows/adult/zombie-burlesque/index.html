What is the show?

What is Zombie Burlesque?

Zombie Burlesque is a 75-minute adult comedy variety show at V Theater inside Miracle Mile Shops. The premise imagines a campy version of 1950s Las Vegas populated by zombies, then uses that setup to mix burlesque, specialty acts, comedy and live music.

The zombie theme is campy and comic, giving the show permission to bounce between musical numbers, physical comedy and strange variety acts. That deliberately ridiculous setup is what gives Zombie Burlesque its offbeat personality.

The 30-second answer

Is Zombie Burlesque worth seeing?

Yes if you want an adult variety show that plays its zombie premise for laughs, with burlesque, specialty acts and live music.

Zombie Burlesque imagines a version of 1950s Las Vegas where the undead somehow opened a nightclub. Burlesque numbers, physical comedy, specialty acts and live music all fit inside that deliberately campy setup.
