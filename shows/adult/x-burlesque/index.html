What is the show?

What is X Burlesque?

X Burlesque is a 75-minute adults-only topless revue at Bugsy’s Cabaret inside Flamingo Las Vegas. The show combines choreographed dance, lingerie-inspired costuming and contemporary music in a compact production built around classic Vegas burlesque presentation.

It is a straightforward revue rather than a story-driven adult show. The choreography and changing numbers provide the structure, making it a good fit for visitors who specifically want a traditional topless Vegas production without a long runtime or elaborate narrative.

The 30-second answer

Is X Burlesque worth seeing?

Yes if you want a straightforward 18+ Vegas topless revue in a compact 75-minute format at Flamingo.

Provocative, daring and exciting, X Burlesque delivers a steamy, topless X-perience inside the intimate Bugsy's Cabaret at Flamingo Las Vegas. A gorgeous troupe of dancers performs to a selection of hot contemporary music in lingerie inspired by classic Vegas burlesque — with audience interaction woven into every act.

Known as Las Vegas's hottest topless revue for more than a decade, the award-winning 75-minute female revue ranges from playfully flirty to flat-out X-rated. Highlights include the signature "Champagne Wishes" segment — a classic striptease to Michael Bublé's "Fever" performed in a traditional burlesque champagne glass — plus pole acrobatics, feather boas, bathtubs, cowgirls, 70s mod and much more.

Frequently asked questions

Is X Burlesque appropriate for everyone?
No — X Burlesque is a topless adult revue strictly for guests 18 and older. A valid government-issued ID is required at the door. No children, no exceptions. The content ranges from flirty to X-rated.
Where should I sit for X Burlesque?
Bugsy’s Cabaret is intimate enough that every section works. VIP and Pink areas put you closer to the stage and central pole, while General Admission wraps the outer sides of the room.
Can I take photos or video?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography, and no video during the performance.
Where exactly is X Burlesque at the Flamingo?
X Burlesque is performed in Bugsy's Cabaret inside Flamingo Las Vegas. Follow signs from the Flamingo casino floor to the showroom — staff can direct you if needed. Arrive 15–20 minutes early to settle in.
Why does Friday and Saturday have two shows?
The 7:00 PM show works well if you have dinner or other plans afterward. The 10:00 PM late show tends to draw a more energetic crowd. Both shows are the same 75-minute production.
Is there a dress code?
No formal dress code. Vegas cocktail casual is appropriate and feels right in the Flamingo's cabaret setting. Smart casual or dressy works well — just avoid beachwear or overly casual attire.
How much are X Burlesque tickets?
Tickets currently start at $82. Other price points may be available.
