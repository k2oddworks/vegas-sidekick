What is the show?

What is Tournament of Kings?

Tournament of Kings is a 75-minute medieval dinner show inside King Arthur’s Arena at Excalibur. Live horses, jousting, sword combat and fire effects happen around the arena while guests eat a medieval-style meal included with the ticket.

The room is part of the show: seating wraps around the performance area and each section has a knight to cheer for, turning the audience into part of the atmosphere without requiring anyone to go onstage. It is equal parts dinner theater, live stunt show and family spectacle.

The 30-second answer

Is Tournament of Kings worth seeing?

Yes if your group wants dinner and spectacle in the same booking, with horses, jousting and sword combat happening around the arena.

Frequently asked questions

Is dinner included with Tournament of Kings?
Yes. The current meal includes Cornish hen, potatoes, corn, a biscuit and apple-square dessert, with nonalcoholic beverages included. Premium drinks are separate.
Do you get utensils at Tournament of Kings?
The meal is intentionally served without utensils as part of the medieval theme. Guests who need utensils for accessibility can ask the staff for accommodation.
What is the age policy for Tournament of Kings?
All ages are welcome. Children 3 and under may attend free while sitting on an adult's lap and sharing the adult's meal. Guests 4 and older need their own ticket. Guests under 16 should be accompanied by an adult.
Where should I sit at Tournament of Kings?
The seats run five rows around the arena. Use the chart to choose the named country section on the side you prefer.
How much are Tournament of Kings tickets?
Tickets currently start at $78. Your date and seating section determine the final total.
How long is Tournament of Kings?
Plan on about 75 minutes. Confirm the current runtime if your exact date matters to the rest of your night.
