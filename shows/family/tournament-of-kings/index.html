The 30-second answer

Is Tournament of Kings worth seeing?

Live jousting, trained horses, sword combat and a medieval-style dinner served around the arena at Excalibur.

Real horses, live jousting, fire effects and a full medieval meal served while your section screams for its knight. This is dinner theater with volume.

Tournament of Kings puts the audience around a central arena floor, divides the room into kingdoms and gives each section a knight to cheer for. The performance mixes horse work, jousting, sword combat and fire effects while dinner is served directly to your seat.

🌵
Kris’s take

This is an easy family pick when dinner solving part of the night is a feature. It may be a weaker fit if you want a quiet meal or traditional theater—the arena is supposed to be loud and participatory.

Frequently asked questions

Is dinner included with Tournament of Kings?
Yes. The current meal includes Cornish hen, potatoes, corn, a biscuit and apple-square dessert, with nonalcoholic beverages included. Premium drinks are separate.
Do you get utensils at Tournament of Kings?
The meal is intentionally served without utensils as part of the medieval theme. Guests who need utensils for accessibility can ask the staff for accommodation.
What is the age policy for Tournament of Kings?
All ages are welcome. Children 3 and under may attend free while sitting on an adult's lap and sharing the adult's meal. Guests 4 and older need their own ticket. Guests under 16 should be accompanied by an adult.
Where should I sit at Tournament of Kings?
Romania and Dragon offer a more direct center view of the arena. Spain and Austria give a side angle. The arena has only a few rows, so even farther-back seats remain close to the action.
How much are Tournament of Kings tickets?
Tickets currently start at $74. Your date and seating section determine the final total.
How long is Tournament of Kings?
Plan on about 75 minutes. Confirm the current runtime if your exact date matters to the rest of your night.
