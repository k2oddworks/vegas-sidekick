What is the show?

What is BattleBots: Destruct-A-Thon?

BattleBots: Destruct-A-Thon is a 75-minute live robot-combat show at the BattleBots Arena just off the Strip. Full-size fighting robots compete in the arena using the same basic kind of destructive match format that made the television series popular.

The attraction is the competition itself: machines built to hit, flip and damage one another in front of a live crowd. Recognizable BattleBots names can be part of the action, but this is not a stage adaptation with actors pretending to fight robots—the robotic combat is the show.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the BattleBots Arena located?
The BattleBots Arena is at 4165 Koval Lane, right behind Horseshoe Las Vegas (formerly Bally's). It is NOT on the Strip itself but is a quick walk or short rideshare from the center of the Strip. Plan to arrive 20-30 minutes before showtime to park, find the venue, and get to your seats.
Is BattleBots: Destruct-A-Thon good for kids?
Yes — BattleBots has no age restriction and is one of the most genuinely exciting all-ages shows in Las Vegas. Toddlers 2 and under are admitted free as long as they sit on a lap and do not require their own seat. Kids who watch the TV show will be blown away seeing their favorite robots in person.
Which robots from the TV show will I see?
The Destruct-A-Thon features robots from the BattleBots Discovery series — including Witch Doctor, Kraken, Mammoth, HyperShock, and Whiplash among others, alongside new challengers. The specific robot lineup may vary by show date. All competing robots are championship-level machines built and driven by the same builders you see on television.
What time does BattleBots start and how long is the show?
BattleBots: Destruct-A-Thon runs Wednesday and Thursday at 6:00 PM and Friday through Sunday at 2:00 PM. The show is dark Monday and Tuesday. The performance is 75 minutes with no intermission. Arrive at least 20 minutes before showtime.
Can I take photos or video during BattleBots?
No. The use of cameras and audio/video recording devices is not permitted during the show. This is strictly enforced. Put the phone away and watch — robot combat at this level is something best experienced live with your full attention on the arena.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for Las Vegas live entertainment. In the event of a production cancellation, refund options will be provided. Review terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
