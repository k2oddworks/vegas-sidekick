The 30-second answer

Is V – The Ultimate Variety Show worth seeing?

One 75-minute show, several different kinds of acts: acrobatics, comedy, magic, juggling, roller skating and rotating specialty performances.

The variety format is the point. Instead of asking a mixed-age group to agree on one style of show, V moves from act to act and keeps the night changing. The rotating lineup also means the exact mix can evolve while the overall format stays familiar.

Frequently asked questions

Where is V Theater at Planet Hollywood?
V Theater is inside the Miracle Mile Shops at Planet Hollywood on the Las Vegas Strip. The listed address is 3667 S Las Vegas Blvd. Give yourself enough time to navigate the shops before the performance.
Is V – The Ultimate Variety Show appropriate for kids?
Yes. There are no age restrictions. Children ages 2 and under may sit on a parent’s lap without their own seat; older children need a ticket.
How often does the V show lineup change?
The show uses a rotating variety format, so individual specialty acts can change while the core mix of acrobatics, comedy, magic, juggling and other variety performances remains.
Which seats should I get for V – The Ultimate Variety Show?
VIP Seating is the Vegas Sidekick Sweet Spot / Our Pick. It wraps the stage across the center and sides and keeps you close to the rotating specialty acts. General Admission sits farther back and to the sides with a wider view of the full stage.
How long is V – The Ultimate Variety Show?
The current listed runtime is 75 minutes.
When does V – The Ultimate Variety Show perform?
The current listed schedule is daily at 7 PM. Confirm the exact date and time during checkout.
