Frequently asked questions

Where is V Theater at Planet Hollywood?
V Theater is located inside the Miracle Mile Shops at Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino on the Las Vegas Strip. Planet Hollywood is at 3667 S Las Vegas Blvd. Once inside Miracle Mile Shops, follow the signs to V Theater. Plan to arrive 20–30 minutes before showtime to find parking, navigate the shops, and get seated.
Is V – The Ultimate Variety Show appropriate for kids?
Yes — V is one of the best family shows in Las Vegas. Children 3 and under are admitted free on a parent's lap. Children 4 and older require their own seat. The show has no adult content — it's acrobatics, juggling, comedy, magic, and roller skating. Kids tend to love it.
How often does the V show lineup change?
V rotates acts periodically to keep the show fresh. The core format — world-class variety acts spanning acrobatics, comedy, magic, juggling, and roller skating — remains consistent, but specific performers and acts change. If you've seen V before, there's a good chance you'll see at least one new act.
What are Preferred seats at V Theater, and why does Spike recommend them?
Preferred seats occupy the center rows of V Theater — far enough from the stage to see the full picture of every act, close enough to feel the energy and catch the facial expressions and precision detail work. For a variety show with multiple act types (aerial, ground, comedy, roller skating), a center seat gives you the ideal vantage for all of them. VIP seats up front are great but can require looking up for aerial acts. Preferred is the balanced choice.
How long is V – The Ultimate Variety Show?
V runs approximately 75 minutes with no intermission. Plan to arrive 20 minutes early to find your seats.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for Las Vegas live entertainment. V – The Ultimate Variety Show has been running continuously for over 20 years — show cancellations are rare. In the event of a production cancellation, refund options will be provided.
