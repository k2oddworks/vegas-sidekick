What is the show?

What is Michael Jackson ONE by Cirque du Soleil?

Michael Jackson ONE by Cirque du Soleil is a 90-minute production at Mandalay Bay built around Michael Jackson’s original recordings, dance, acrobatics and 360-degree staging. It celebrates the music without turning the night into a traditional impersonator concert.

The performers handle the choreography, aerial work and visual storytelling while Jackson’s catalogue remains the center of the show. That makes ONE part concert tribute and part Cirque spectacle, with the music carrying the emotional through-line and the staging expanding it into a full theater production.

The 30-second answer

Is Michael Jackson ONE worth seeing?

Yes if Michael Jackson’s music is the main draw and you want it wrapped in Cirque-scale dance, acrobatics and staging.

The smartest part of the concept is that it does not turn into an impersonator show. Michael’s music stays at the center while the cast handles the movement, aerial work and visual storytelling around it.

Frequently asked questions

Does someone play Michael Jackson in the show?
No. The production uses Michael Jackson’s original recordings rather than casting an impersonator.
What age is appropriate for Michael Jackson ONE?
Everyone must be 5 years of age or older to attend the event.
What nights does Michael Jackson ONE run?
The regular weekly schedule is Monday and Thursday through Sunday at 7:00 PM and 9:30 PM. Tuesday and Wednesday are dark.
How long is Michael Jackson ONE?
The current listed runtime is 90 minutes.
Where should I sit for Michael Jackson ONE?
Vegas Sidekick’s Sweet Spot / Our Pick includes sections 101, 102, 103, 202, 203 and 204. The side upper sections 201 and 205 give you a wider look at the full production.
