The 30-second answer

Is Michael Jackson ONE by Cirque du Soleil worth seeing?

Michael Jackson ONE by Cirque du Soleil at Mandalay Bay Theatre features original MJ recordings, 63 performers, acrobatics, and 360-degree staging.

Michael Jackson ONE has been running at Mandalay Bay since 2013 — and it has never stopped being one of the best shows in Las Vegas. Cirque du Soleil built this production from scratch using Michael's original recordings, his movement vocabulary, and the emotional weight of his catalog. The result is something you don't get anywhere else on the Strip.

The show follows four misfit characters pulled into Michael's world across 90 minutes of acrobatics, dance, and production design that's technically unlike anything else in town. There are 587 lighting fixtures. 26 video mapping projectors. A custom-built theatre with 360-degree staging so the action surrounds you rather than just playing out in front of you.

Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Does someone play Michael Jackson in the show?
No. Cirque du Soleil made a deliberate decision not to cast an MJ impersonator. The show uses Michael's original voice and recordings, and 63 performers embody his energy and movement style without pretending to be him. It's a smarter approach — and a more powerful one.
What age is appropriate for Michael Jackson ONE?
The show is open to guests ages 5 and up. It's loud, visually intense, and moves fast — most kids comfortable in a theatre setting will love it. If your child is sensitive to loud music or sudden lighting changes, that's worth knowing in advance.
Which show time is better — 7PM or 9:30PM?
Both are the same show. The 7PM works better for families or earlier dinners. The 9:30PM tends to draw a livelier crowd. Pick what fits your schedule.
What nights does the show run?
Michael Jackson ONE runs Thursday through Monday with two shows nightly at 7:00 PM and 9:30 PM. The show is dark on Tuesday and Wednesday. Always verify your specific date at checkout.
How long is the show?
Approximately 90 minutes with no intermission. Use the restroom before doors open. Plan to arrive at Mandalay Bay at least 30 minutes before showtime.
