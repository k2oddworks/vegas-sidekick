Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Will I actually get splashed?
The splash zone in the front rows can get a light misting during certain sequences — it's part of the experience. If you're in the first few rows, expect it. If you'd rather stay dry, choose seats in the lower orchestra center or further back. The theatre will let you know at booking if your seats are in the splash zone.
Is "O" appropriate for kids?
Yes — ages 5 and up are welcome. "O" is visually stunning and moves fast enough to hold most kids' attention. It's not loud or scary — just spectacular. All guests under 18 must be accompanied by an adult.
What nights does "O" run?
"O" runs Wednesday through Sunday with two shows nightly at 6:30 PM and 9:00 PM. The show is dark Monday and Tuesday. Always verify your specific date at checkout.
Which show time is better — 6:30PM or 9:00PM?
Both are identical shows. The 6:30PM works better for families or if you want the evening ahead of you. The 9:00PM has a slightly livelier crowd energy — and the Bellagio fountains are running when you walk out. Spike recommends the late show.
Are there good seats and bad seats?
Every seat has a clear view of the pool and stage. Lower orchestra center is the sweet spot. The balcony offers a unique bird's-eye perspective that some guests actually prefer for the synchronized swimming sequences. No bad seats — just different experiences.
How long is the show and when should I arrive?
"O" runs approximately 90 minutes with no intermission, so use the restroom before doors open. Seating begins about 30 minutes before showtime, but plan to arrive at the Bellagio 45 minutes before showtime — that's enough time to find the O Theatre, hit the restroom, and grab a drink before you sit down.
Where should I park at the Bellagio?
The Bellagio has a self-parking garage attached to the property. Rideshare on Las Vegas Blvd is the smoothest option. Since seating opens about 30 minutes before showtime, plan to arrive at the Bellagio 45 minutes before showtime so you have time to park, walk in, and find the O Theatre without rushing.
