What is the show?

What is KÀ?

KÀ by Cirque du Soleil is a 90-minute story-driven production at MGM Grand built around one of the most unusual stages in Las Vegas. The massive performance surface can move, tilt and change position, turning the theater itself into part of the action.

Aerial work, acrobatics and combat-style choreography are woven into a clearer narrative than many Cirque shows. The technical staging is the signature: performers climb, fight and move across surfaces that do not behave like a normal flat stage, giving KÀ a distinctly cinematic scale.

The 30-second answer

Is KÀ worth seeing?

Yes if you want Cirque at its most cinematic — a giant moving stage, action-heavy choreography and an adventure story driving the 90 minutes.

The theater is a major part of the experience. The central platform rises, tilts and can become a near-vertical performance surface, turning the stage engineering into part of the spectacle.

Frequently asked questions

How much are KÀ tickets?

Tickets start at $80. Other price points may be available.

How long is KÀ?

KÀ runs about 90 minutes with no intermission.

What days does KÀ perform?

The regular schedule is Monday through Wednesday at 6:00 PM and 8:30 PM, plus Saturday and Sunday at 4:30 PM and 7:00 PM. Thursday and Friday are dark. Check your exact date before purchasing.

Is KÀ good for kids?

Guests must be at least 3 years old. Guests under 18 must attend with an adult.

Where is the KÀ Theatre?

KÀ Theatre is inside MGM Grand at 3799 S Las Vegas Blvd. MGM Grand is large, so allow extra time to reach the theater.

Where should I sit for KÀ?

Sections 101–103 and 202 are the Vegas Sidekick Sweet Spot / Our Pick. The lower 100-level sections sit closer to the stage, while centered section 202 gives you a higher view of the full moving platform.
