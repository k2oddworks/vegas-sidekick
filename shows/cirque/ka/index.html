What is the show?

What is KÀ?

KÀ by Cirque du Soleil is a 90-minute story-driven production at MGM Grand built around one of the most unusual stages in Las Vegas. The massive performance surface can move, tilt and change position, turning the theater itself into part of the action.

Aerial work, acrobatics and combat-style choreography are woven into a more defined narrative than many Cirque shows. The technical staging is the signature: performers climb, fight and move across spaces that do not behave like a normal flat stage, giving KÀ a distinctly cinematic scale.

The 30-second answer

Is KÀ worth seeing?

Yes if you want Cirque at its most cinematic — a giant moving stage, martial-arts choreography and a real adventure story instead of a loose collection of acts.

KÀ is one of the Cirque shows where the theater matters almost as much as the performers. The central platform rises, tilts and turns into a near-vertical wall while the cast fights, climbs and falls across it. The production is more narrative than most Cirque shows, following twins separated during an attack and moving through a fantasy adventure.

🌵 Kris’s take

If someone says “I want Cirque” and wants something dramatic, KÀ is an easy place to start. It is more serious and story-driven than the lighter Cirque shows; Mystère is the better comparison if you want act-after-act acrobatics with less plot.

Before you go

KÀ FAQ

Tickets start at $80. Other price points may be available.
KÀ runs about 90 minutes with no intermission.
The regular schedule is Monday through Wednesday at 6:00 PM and 8:30 PM, plus Saturday and Sunday at 4:30 PM and 7:00 PM. Thursday and Friday are dark. Confirm your exact date at checkout.
Guests must be at least 3 years old. Guests under 18 should attend with an adult. The show is highly visual, though some younger children may find the battle sequences intense.
KÀ Theatre is inside MGM Grand at 3799 S Las Vegas Blvd. MGM Grand is large, so allow extra time to reach the theater.
