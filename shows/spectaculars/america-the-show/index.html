What is the show?

What is America The Show?

America The Show is a 75-minute Las Vegas production at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort built around American music and classic showmanship. Rather than following one singer or one musical genre, it uses a patriotic theme to move through songs and stage presentation connected to different parts of American popular entertainment.

The show leans into the kind of music-led revue format that used to be common in Las Vegas: costumes, performance and a broad theme tying the night together. It is aimed at people who want a traditional stage production rather than a tribute to one specific artist.

Frequently asked questions

Where is America The Show performed?
America The Show plays at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort, 375 E Harmon Ave — just off the Strip, a short drive or rideshare east of the main action. Alexis Park is a no-casino resort, so it's a quieter, easier-in-and-out venue with free parking. Plan to arrive about 30 minutes before showtime to get seated.
Who is America The Show for?
Yes — America The Show is an all-ages show. It's a feel-good Motown tribute with legendary songs, sparkling costumes, and lots of dancing. Bring the whole family, bring your parents, bring a group of friends — everyone in the room already knows the words.
Which seats are best for America The Show?
The front rows put you closest to the stage, the center tables give you a straight-on view with table seating, and the side seating gives you a side angle.
Can I take photos during the show?
Still photos may be allowed — check with your usher first before the show starts. As a courtesy to the performers and other guests, avoid flash and video during the performance. When in doubt, ask staff at the door.
What are the showtimes?
America The Show runs Saturday and Sunday at 7:00 PM. Weekends only. Always confirm the exact date and time at checkout before you book.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a production cancellation, you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
