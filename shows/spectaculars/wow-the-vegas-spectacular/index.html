What is the show?

What is WOW – The Vegas Spectacular?

WOW – The Vegas Spectacular is a 90-minute production at Rio built around a custom water stage, acrobatics, aerial performance, roller skating and specialty acts. The show uses a broad visual-variety format rather than relying on one star performer or one continuous story.

Water effects and physical performance are the connective tissue. One sequence can focus on aerial work while another shifts to skating or acrobatics, giving the production the feel of a large-scale visual sampler designed to keep changing throughout the night.

Frequently asked questions

Where is WOW – The Vegas Spectacular located?
WOW performs at the Rio Showroom inside Rio Hotel &amp; Casino at 3700 W Flamingo Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89103. The Rio is about a mile west of the Strip. Complimentary valet and self-parking are available on site. Plan to arrive 20–30 minutes before showtime to park and get to your seats.
Is WOW – The Vegas Spectacular appropriate for kids?
Yes. WOW is recommended for ages 3 and up and is one of the most family-friendly water spectaculars in Las Vegas. All patrons — including children — must have a ticket. Infants under 1 year old may be held on the lap of a paying ticket holder. The show contains no adult content and thrills audiences of all ages.
Are photos or videos allowed at WOW?
No. WOW – The Vegas Spectacular has a strict no-photo, no-video policy throughout the entire performance. This applies to phones, cameras, and all recording devices. Please respect the policy and the performers.
How long is WOW – The Vegas Spectacular?
WOW runs approximately 90 minutes with no intermission. Plan to arrive at least 20 minutes early to find your seats and get settled.
What makes WOW different from other Las Vegas acrobatic shows?
WOW is built around a custom water stage with towering water walls — water is the central element of the entire production, not just a backdrop. The intimate 180-degree theater at the Rio puts every seat just feet from the splash. With 30 elite performers spanning acrobatics, aerial arts, roller skating, and comedy, and a track record of over 3,000 shows since 2017, WOW delivers Cirque-level production values at a fraction of the price.
How is seating arranged for WOW – The Vegas Spectacular?
The supplied seating chart wraps the room around the center stage. Premium Center and the numbered inner seating face the middle, with Premium Front, Premium Side, Preferred Side and Reserved Side sections arranged around both sides of the room.
What is the cancellation policy for WOW tickets?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, standard for Las Vegas live shows. WOW has been performing since 2017 with over 3,000 shows completed — production cancellations are rare. In the event of a cancellation, refund options will be provided. Review terms at checkout.
