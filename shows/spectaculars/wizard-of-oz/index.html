What is the show?

What is The Wizard of Oz at Sphere?

The Wizard of Oz at Sphere is a 75-minute immersive cinema experience that reworks the 1939 MGM film for the scale and technology of Sphere Las Vegas. Large-format visuals fill the venue while spatial audio, haptic seats and atmospheric effects extend the movie beyond the screen.

This is not a conventional stage musical or a simple movie screening. The familiar story is the foundation, but the attraction is how Sphere turns it into a physical experience with effects such as movement, wind, mist and other environmental cues synchronized to what is happening onscreen.

The 30-second answer

Is The Wizard of Oz at Sphere worth seeing?

Yes if you want the Sphere itself to be part of the attraction: a familiar story rebuilt around a massive screen, haptic seats and physical effects.

This is a 75-minute version of The Wizard of Oz made specifically for Sphere. The experience layers the movie with spatial audio, seat haptics and atmospheric effects such as wind, mist, scent and pyrotechnics, so the venue is doing as much work as the story.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the actual 1939 Wizard of Oz film?
It’s the complete story — Dorothy, Oz, all of it — but the runtime is 75 minutes, shorter than the original film. Some scenes have been condensed or trimmed for the Sphere experience. The original vocal performances are preserved, the visuals are remastered for Sphere, and the score has been re-recorded for the venue’s spatial audio system.
What exactly are the haptic seats?
The experience uses infrasound haptic technology in the seating, with low-frequency vibrations timed to moments in the film so you can physically feel effects such as the tornado and thunder.
Can I bring young kids?
The Wizard of Oz at Sphere is intended for guests ages 6+. All guests, regardless of age, require a ticket.
What if I’m sensitive to motion or sensory effects?
This immersive experience may include seat haptics, movement sensations, flashing or intense lighting, loud noises, flying or falling objects, pyrotechnics, fog, scent, mist and wind. Those elements may aggravate some medical or physical conditions. Guests with concerns, including guests who are pregnant, should consult a physician before attending.
Is there late seating?
No. The event information states that late seating is not permitted, so arrive before the time printed on your ticket.
Where is Sphere?
Sphere is at 255 Sands Ave, just east of The Venetian and The Palazzo. Follow the access and ticket-delivery instructions included with your order.
Can I get a refund if my plans change?
The current event terms list tickets as non-refundable. Review the purchase terms before you book.
