What is the show?

What is Laugh Factory Las Vegas?

Laugh Factory Las Vegas is a 75-minute rotating stand-up show at Horseshoe Las Vegas. The club books changing comedians and offers several nightly showtimes, so the exact lineup can be different depending on when you visit.

The format is intentionally simple: comics, a microphone and a club audience. That makes it different from Vegas comedy productions built around props, magic or hypnosis. If you want a traditional stand-up night with the possibility of seeing a different bill on a return visit, that is the point.

The 30-second answer

Is Laugh Factory Las Vegas worth seeing?

Yes if you want a classic comedy-club format with a rotating lineup and multiple late-day showtime options.

The lineup has rotated since your last visit — it always does. That's the whole model. Check who's on the bill this week and come back. Three shows every night means you've got options on timing too.

The Laugh Factory name goes back to 1979 in Hollywood — it's where Richard Pryor, Jim Carrey, and Jerry Seinfeld built their careers. The Las Vegas version brings the same rotating headliner format to Horseshoe Las Vegas on the Strip, two shows a night, every night. Real stand-up in a room built for it, with a name that means something in comedy.

Frequently asked questions

Which show should I book — 8:30 PM or 10:30 PM?
Both shows run the same 70-minute format but with different lineups. The 8:30 PM show works naturally after a Strip dinner and fits an earlier evening. The 10:30 PM show runs later and typically draws a crowd that's already been out — the energy tends to run looser and the comics know it. Check the bill for each show before deciding.
Who are the comedians performing?
The lineup rotates regularly — that's the Laugh Factory model. The bill features a mix of established headliners and rising talent. Check the current schedule before purchasing to see who's on stage the night you're attending.
Can I take photos during the show?
No. No photos or video during the show. Stow your device when the show starts and watch the comedy. This is standard practice at serious stand-up clubs and it's enforced here.
What is the age requirement?
Laugh Factory Las Vegas is strictly 18 and older. No exceptions. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID — it will be checked at the door. Guests who cannot verify their age will not be admitted.
Where is Horseshoe Las Vegas on the Strip?
Horseshoe Las Vegas is located at 3645 S Las Vegas Blvd, between Flamingo Road and the Bellagio on the central Strip. Previously known as Bally's Las Vegas. Easily walkable from Paris Las Vegas, Caesars, and the Bellagio. Rideshare drop-off is straightforward off Las Vegas Boulevard.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Refunds are available for exceptional circumstances such as the unlikely event that a show cancels. Check the full terms and conditions before purchasing.
