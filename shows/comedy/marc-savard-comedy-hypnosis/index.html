Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to volunteer to be hypnotized?
Not at all. Volunteering is completely optional. Marc will invite anyone who's interested to raise their hand at the start of the show — that's the extent of any pressure. You decide whether to participate. Plenty of people come specifically to watch, and they have just as good a night as anyone who steps on stage.
What if I volunteer but change my mind?
You can step back at any point. Raising your hand is expressing interest — it's not a commitment. Marc runs a brief group induction at the start, and the volunteers who respond most naturally are the ones who remain on stage. If it doesn't feel right, sitting back down is completely fine and happens without any awkwardness. The whole show is built around people who genuinely want to be there.
Is the hypnosis real?
The volunteers are real audience members — not actors, not pre-screened plants. Marc Savard has 30 years of full-time professional hypnosis and over 7,500 live performances behind him. The show works because hypnosis works on people who are genuinely open to it, and Marc's experience means he knows how to identify and guide the right volunteers in real time. Skeptics who talk to participants after the show tend to leave quieter than when they started.
Is this show appropriate for kids and families?
Yes — Marc Savard Comedy Hypnosis is an all-ages show. There's some mild adult humor woven in, but nothing gratuitous or inappropriate for families. Children ages 2 and under may sit on a lap at no charge. Everyone attending needs to be accompanied by an adult.
Where is the show and how long does it run?
The show performs at the Nathan Burton Theater inside Showcase Mall at 3771 S Las Vegas Blvd — right on the Strip. It runs 70 minutes with no intermission. Doors typically open about 30 minutes before showtime. Paid parking is available in the Showcase Mall parking garage off Las Vegas Boulevard.
When does the show run?
Marc Savard Comedy Hypnosis performs Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday at 7:00 PM. The show is dark on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Always verify the current schedule before purchasing.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Refunds are available for exceptional circumstances such as the unlikely event that a show cancels. Check the full terms and conditions before purchasing.
