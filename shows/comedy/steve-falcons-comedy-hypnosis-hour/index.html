Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Where is Steve Falcon's Comedy Hypnosis Hour performed?
The show plays at the Underground Theater inside the MGM Grand Hotel & Casino, 3799 S Las Vegas Blvd, at the south end of the Strip. Enter through the main MGM Grand casino and follow signage to the theater. The MGM Grand is enormous, so plan to arrive at the resort 45 minutes before showtime to give yourself time to walk to the theater, hit the restroom, and grab a drink.
Is Steve Falcon's Comedy Hypnosis Hour an adult show?
Yes. This is an R-rated adult comedy show — expect strong language, adult humor, and unscripted moments. Guests must be 16 years or older, and anyone under 18 must be accompanied by an adult. It's a late-night party atmosphere built for grown-ups looking to laugh.
Do I have to get hypnotized?
No. Only willing audience volunteers are hypnotized and become part of the show — participation is voluntary. If you'd rather just watch and laugh from your seat, that works too. And because the volunteers are different every night, no two performances are ever the same.
What are the showtimes?
The show runs seven nights a week at the Underground Theater. Showtime is 9:30 PM Sunday through Saturday, with an earlier 8:00 PM start on Wednesdays. Always confirm the exact date and time at checkout before you book.
How long is the show?
Steve Falcon's Comedy Hypnosis Hour runs 85 minutes with no intermission. It's a fast-paced, high-energy set that moves quickly from the moment Steve hits the stage.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a production cancellation, you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
