What is the show?

What is Carrot Top?

Carrot Top is a 75-minute comedy residency at Luxor built around handmade props, visual gags and rapid-fire punchlines. Instead of standing at a microphone and stretching one story into a long bit, he keeps cycling through physical jokes, new props and short setups.

That pace is the defining feature. The show moves quickly enough that one idea rarely sits around for long, and the comedy is designed to be seen as much as heard. It is a strong fit for people who want something more visual and kinetic than a traditional stand-up set.

The 30-second answer

Is Carrot Top worth seeing?

Yes if fast visual jokes and handmade props sound better to you than a stand-up set built around long stories or crowd work.

Carrot Top fires through props and rapid visual jokes, so the format keeps changing before any one bit has time to wear out. The 75-minute runtime also makes it easy to build the rest of a Vegas night around the show.

Frequently asked questions

How much are Carrot Top tickets in Las Vegas?
Tickets currently start at $62. Other price points may be available depending on the performance and seating section.
Which seats should I buy for Carrot Top?
Reserved 2 is the Vegas Sidekick Sweet Spot / Our Pick for Carrot Top. It gives you the best balance of stage detail and the full-room view. Reserved 1 is closest to the stage, while Reserved 3 and Reserved 4 sit farther back.
How long is the Carrot Top show?
Carrot Top runs about 75 minutes.
What days and times does Carrot Top perform?
The regular schedule is Monday through Saturday at 8 PM. Some dates can differ, so check the available dates and times for your trip.
Is Carrot Top appropriate for teenagers?
Must be 16 years of age or older. Guests 18 years of age and under must be accompanied by an adult 21+.
Where is the Carrot Top show?
Luxor Las Vegas, inside the Atrium Showroom at 3900 S Las Vegas Blvd. Allow time to get from parking or rideshare to the showroom. Aim to reach the property 45 minutes before the show; late seating is not guaranteed.
Does a meet-and-greet include the show ticket?
No. The meet-and-greet is a separate add-on and does not include admission to the show. Check availability and the arrival instructions before purchasing either ticket.
