Is Carrot Top worth seeing?

The show is built around visual props, quick punchlines and crowd interaction. You can walk in cold and keep up.

Yes, especially if you like comedy that keeps moving. Carrot Top fires through handmade props, rapid visual jokes and audience moments, so there is always something new happening onstage. It is easy to follow, fast on its feet and built to get laughs without making you wait through a long setup. Watch the trailer for a quick read on the pace.

Before you book

Carrot Top tickets: your questions answered

How much are Carrot Top tickets in Las Vegas?

Tickets start at $62. The final price varies by date and seating section, so compare the live options before booking.

Which seats should I buy for Carrot Top?

Center section is my Sweet Spot for a straight-on stage view. Front rows put you closest to the action; farther back gives you more distance. Use the live seat map on the booking screen to compare rows and prices.

How long is the Carrot Top show?

About 75 minutes with no intermission. For an 8 PM performance, plan on finishing around 9:15 PM, plus time to leave the showroom.

What days and times does Carrot Top perform?

The typical schedule is Monday through Saturday at 8 PM. Some dates are dark and extra performances are added from time to time, so check the booking screen for the live dates and times.

Is Carrot Top appropriate for teenagers?

Guests must be 16 years of age or older. Guests 18 years of age and under must be accompanied by an adult age 21 or older. Expect adult humor; this is not a show for younger children.

Where is the Carrot Top show?

Luxor Las Vegas, inside the Atrium Showroom at 3900 S Las Vegas Blvd. Allow time to get from parking or rideshare to the showroom. Aim to reach the property 45 minutes before the show; late seating is not guaranteed.

Does a meet-and-greet include the show ticket?

No. The meet-and-greet is a separate add-on and does not include admission to the show. Check availability and the arrival instructions before purchasing either ticket.
