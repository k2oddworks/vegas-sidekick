The 30-second answer

Is Carrot Top worth seeing?

The show is built around visual props, quick punchlines and crowd interaction. You can walk in cold and keep up.

Yes, if prop comedy is your thing. The appeal is the constant switch from one visual gag to the next, rather than a long story with one payoff. The tradeoff: if you find props distracting, that is the format for the whole night. Watch the trailer before paying for a seat.

Before you book

Carrot Top tickets: your questions answered

How much are Carrot Top tickets in Las Vegas?

Tickets start at $62 with our ticket partner, checked September 6, 2026. The final price depends on your date and seat. Review the full total and purchase terms before paying.

Which seats should I buy for Carrot Top?

Center section is my Sweet Spot for a straight-on stage view. Front rows put you closest to the action; farther back gives you more distance. Use the actual seat map at booking to compare rows and prices. Our diagram is an illustrative guide, not a live seating chart.

How long is the Carrot Top show?

About 75 minutes with no intermission. For an 8 PM performance, plan on finishing around 9:15 PM, plus time to leave the showroom.

What days and times does Carrot Top perform?

Usually Monday through Saturday at 8 PM. Luxor also lists dark weeks and selected Sunday shows, so the regular weekly pattern is not a guarantee for your date. Check the performance calendar before booking.

Is Carrot Top appropriate for teenagers?

The minimum age is 16. Guests 18 and under must be accompanied by an adult 21+, and ID is required. Expect adult humor; this is not a show for younger children.

Who handles the ticket booking?

You complete your purchase with our ticket partner. Vegas Sidekick does not sell tickets or collect a booking fee. We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Check the final total, cancellation terms and delivery details before paying.

Where is the Carrot Top show?

The Atrium Showroom inside Luxor, 3900 S Las Vegas Blvd. Allow time to get from parking or rideshare to the showroom. Aim to reach the property 45 minutes before the show; late seating is not guaranteed.

Does a meet-and-greet include the show ticket?

No. The meet-and-greet is a separate add-on and does not include admission to the show. Check availability and the arrival instructions before purchasing either ticket.
