What is Delirious Comedy Club?

Delirious Comedy Club presents stand-up comedy at Silver Sevens Hotel & Casino on Paradise Road in Las Vegas. Instead of one permanent cast, the club features a changing lineup of comedians, so the performance depends on the night you choose.

The setting is a comedy club rather than a large theater production. It's a practical choice if you want to spend an evening watching stand-up, with free parking advertised at the hotel. Check the individual lineup and ticket terms when selecting a performance.
