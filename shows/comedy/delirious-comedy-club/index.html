The show

What is Delirious Comedy Club?

Delirious Comedy Club brings touring stand-up headliners and newer comedy voices to a smaller showroom inside Silver Sevens Hotel & Casino. The lineup changes, so the performer you see depends on the date you book.

It's a straightforward comedy-club night rather than a large theater production: stand-up, a close-up room, and an off-Strip location on Paradise Road. The club advertises drink specials and free parking.

Delirious Comedy Club FAQ

The advertised schedule is Thursday through Sunday at 7 PM, plus 9 PM on Friday and Saturday. Dates can vary; confirm before booking.
