Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Which show should I book — 7 PM or 9:30 PM?
Both shows run the same format — five headliners, 90 minutes — but with different lineups. Check who's on the bill for each show before you decide. As a general rule, the 7 PM show works better for earlier dinners and a fuller evening, while the 9:30 PM show tends to attract a crowd that's already warmed up and wants to run later. Either is a solid choice.
Who are the comedians performing?
The lineup rotates constantly — that's the Comedy Cellar model. Five headlining comedians per show, each doing 15 to 20 minutes of their best material. The bill includes both established names and rising talent. Check the current schedule before purchasing to see who's running the room the night you want to attend.
What is the age requirement?
Comedy Cellar Las Vegas is strictly 18 and older. No exceptions, no matter how mature they seem. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID — it will be checked at the door. Guests who cannot verify their age will not be admitted.
Where is the Rio Hotel from the Strip?
The Rio All-Suite Hotel & Casino is located just west of the Strip at 3700 W Flamingo Rd — about a 10-minute drive or rideshare from the center of Las Vegas Boulevard. It's not on the Strip itself, but rideshare access is easy and the Rio has its own parking structure on property.
How long is the show?
90 minutes with no intermission. Five comedians, 15 to 20 minutes each, back to back. Seating opens 30 minutes before showtime — plan to be at the Rio 45 minutes early so you have time to find the room, use the restroom, and grab a drink before the first comedian hits the stage.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Refunds are available for exceptional circumstances such as the unlikely event that a show cancels. Check the full terms and conditions before purchasing.
