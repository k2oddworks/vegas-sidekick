The 30-second answer

Is Tape Face worth seeing?

Wordless physical comedy built from props, music, facial expressions and audience participation at MGM Grand.

▶ Watch official preview Visual comedy · MGM Grand Tape Face No dialogue, no setup monologues — just props, music, physical comedy and an audience that keeps getting pulled into increasingly strange situations.

Tape Face treats silence as the premise rather than a gimmick he has to escape from. The show communicates through music cues, facial expressions, visual props and physical bits that make sense without needing the audience to share the same language or comedy references.

🌵
Kris’s take

Tape Face is useful when your group has very different comedy tastes or different first languages. Think twice only if what you actually want is verbal stand-up—there is essentially no spoken comedy here.

Is it for you?

Good fit

  • You want laughs and personality to drive the night.
  • You would rather see a performer-led show than a giant visual production.
  • The premise of Tape Face sounds like what your group wants.

Think twice

  • You are really shopping for magic, Cirque or a concert.
  • You want the production design—not the performer—to be the main attraction.
  • A different show category is the real priority for your group.

Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Does Tape Face speak during the show?
No. The show is deliberately wordless and uses physical comedy, music, props and audience interaction instead of dialogue.
When does Tape Face perform?
The regular schedule is Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday at 7:30 PM, with Wednesday dark. Confirm the schedule for your date at checkout.
Is Tape Face appropriate for kids?
Tape Face is an all-ages show. Guests ages 3 and older need a ticket; children 2 and under may be admitted as a lap seat according to the current show policy.
Where should I sit for Tape Face?
The main-floor middle sections are a useful balance because you can read the physical comedy clearly without being right on top of the stage. Closer seats increase the chance of feeling involved in the audience interaction.
How much are Tape Face tickets?
Tickets currently start at $57. Your date and seating section determine the final total.
How long is Tape Face?
Plan on about 75 minutes. Confirm the current runtime if your exact date matters to the rest of your night.
