What is Jimmy Kimmel's Comedy Club?

Jimmy Kimmel’s Comedy Club at The LINQ Promenade is a rotating stand-up venue with changing lineups of working comics. The lineup can include established comics, newer performers and occasional surprise guests, so the exact bill is part of what changes from night to night.

The format is straightforward comedy-club Las Vegas: multiple sets, a live room and stand-up as the entire focus. Jimmy Kimmel created the club and curates its identity; the comics on the nightly lineup are the main event.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Jimmy Kimmel's Comedy Club?
Jimmy Kimmel's Comedy Club is on The LINQ Promenade, the outdoor dining and entertainment walkway between The LINQ Hotel and Flamingo Las Vegas. Follow signage along the Promenade to the club entrance. Plan to arrive about 30 minutes early to check in, grab a drink at the bar, and get settled before the show starts.
Is Jimmy Kimmel's Comedy Club appropriate for kids?
No — this is an 18-and-over show, no children allowed. The lineup is curated for an adult audience, though the material spans a wide range of styles and isn't uniformly hard-edged.
Which seats are best?
GA Seated Preferred is the Vegas Sidekick Sweet Spot / Our Pick. It fills the broad orange center section between Reserved 1 and GA Seated Standard. Reserved 1 wraps closest to the stage, Standard sits on the outer right, and the Premium Booths plus RSVD 2 and RSVD 3 occupy the smaller upper-left blocks.
What should I expect from the show?
A rotating lineup of stand-up comedy personally curated by Jimmy Kimmel and his team — a mix of established comics, promising newcomers, and the occasional surprise guest, aimed at an 18+ audience but with plenty of range for different senses of humor. The dual-level, 8,000-square-foot room mixes traditional seating with long tables for groups, and the bar serves tapas-style bites and craft cocktails before and after the show.
Can I take photos or use my phone during the show?
Still photos may be allowed as long as they aren't distracting — please check with your usher on the way in. No flash photography, and cameras or audio/video recording are not permitted during the performance.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Tickets are generally non-refundable once purchased, which is standard for live Las Vegas entertainment. In the unlikely event of a cancellation you will be notified and refund options will be provided. Review the full terms and conditions at checkout before purchasing.
