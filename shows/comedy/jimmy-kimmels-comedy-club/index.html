About Jimmy Kimmel's Comedy Club

Jimmy Kimmel is bringing his expertise in comedy from the bright lights of Hollywood to his hometown, Las Vegas.

At Jimmy Kimmel's Comedy Club, you'll catch a wide variety of comedic acts from established names to several up-and-coming comedians, meticulously curated and booked by the comedian, producer, writer, and Emmy Award-winning TV host himself. Even on nights Kimmel isn't performing, he and his team are the ones booking the talent — a rotating mix of famous comics, promising newcomers, and the occasional surprise guest. Voted Best Comedy Club in Las Vegas by the Best of Las Vegas awards, the comedy here is aimed at an 18+ audience but covers plenty of range to suit different senses of humor.

🌵
Spike's Take From $27 — no fake "regular price" markup here, just a straightforward ticket to one of the best rooms in town for stand-up. It's a fan-shaped room facing the stage, so seat choice matters. Book somewhere toward the middle of the room, front to back — that gives you a clean, centered view without sitting right against the stage.

The dual-level, 8,000-square-foot venue was personally designed by Kimmel to capture the atmosphere of classic Vegas, mixing traditional seating with long tables for groups of all sizes. Grab a seat at the bar before or after the show for tapas-style shareable bites and craft cocktails. Dates and showtimes vary week to week, so check availability before you book — this is an 18-and-over show, no children allowed.
