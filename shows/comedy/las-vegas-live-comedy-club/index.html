Is Las Vegas LIVE Comedy Club worth seeing?

V Theater (Upstairs · V2) · Miracle Mile Shops · Planet Hollywood · Las Vegas Las Vegas LIVE Comedy Club Rotating Headliners. Every Night. 9 PM.

Las Vegas LIVE Comedy Club runs nightly at 9 PM in the upstairs showroom of the V Theater inside Miracle Mile Shops at Planet Hollywood — a Strip-center location that makes it an easy add to any night out. The format is a rotating lineup of stand-up headliners, 75 minutes, one show a night. Three seating zones give you options from VIP Center up front through the Center and Side sections, all in a room purpose-built for comedy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the upstairs showroom inside Miracle Mile?
Las Vegas LIVE Comedy Club performs in the upstairs showroom at V Theater — on the upper level inside Miracle Mile Shops at Planet Hollywood. Miracle Mile can be a maze, so arriving 15 to 20 minutes early gives you time to navigate and find the room without stress.
Who are the comedians performing?
The lineup rotates — check the current schedule before purchasing to see who's on stage the night you're attending. Las Vegas LIVE brings in a rotating roster of touring stand-up headliners, with the bill changing regularly.
What is the age requirement?
Guests must be 16 years of age or older to attend Las Vegas LIVE Comedy Club. Valid ID may be required at the door.
Can I take photos during the show?
No. The use of cameras and audio/video devices are strictly prohibited during the show. Smoking is also not permitted in the theater. Keep your device stowed once the show begins. This is enforced.
Where is Planet Hollywood on the Strip?
Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino is located at 3667 S Las Vegas Blvd, in the center of the Strip between the Bellagio and Paris Las Vegas. Miracle Mile Shops wraps around the property and can be entered from multiple points along the Boulevard. Rideshare drop-off is straightforward. The property has a self-parking garage accessible from Harmon Avenue.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Refunds are available for exceptional circumstances such as the unlikely event that a show cancels. Check the full terms and conditions before purchasing.
