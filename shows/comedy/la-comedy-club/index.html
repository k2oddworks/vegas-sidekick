What is LA Comedy Club Las Vegas?

LA Comedy Club at The STRAT is a stand-up comedy room with several nightly show formats. The schedule can include an earlier variety-style comedy show, touring comics and a later headliner slot.

That gives you more flexibility than a single-show production. You can choose the time and lineup that fits your night, while the experience stays centered on comics working a live club room at The STRAT.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between the three shows?
Each time slot is a different show. The 6 PM Happy Hour Variety Show is a lighter mix of stand-up and variety acts — a good fit before dinner or earlier in an evening. The 8 PM show brings rotating touring comics, different every week. The 10 PM show features named headliners: Bret Ernst on Tuesday and Wednesday, Butch Bradley Thursday through Monday. Three distinct products in one venue every night.
Who are Bret Ernst and Butch Bradley?
Both are seasoned touring stand-ups with serious credits. Bret Ernst has appeared on Comedy Central and multiple late-night programs; he's known for high-energy storytelling with a sharp edge. Butch Bradley has been a Las Vegas comedy staple for years — he's the kind of comic who knows exactly how to work a room. They have different styles, so knowing which one is on stage the night you're attending is worth a quick check before you buy.
How do the seating areas at LA Comedy Club differ?
The supplied chart shows VIP seating closest to the stage on the left side, General Admission across the main chair seating, four named booths around the room, and six small areas labeled HT1 through HT6 around the outer edges.
What is the age requirement?
LA Comedy Club Las Vegas admits guests ages 15 and older. Guests ages 15 through 17 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian. Valid ID may be required at the door.
Where is The STRAT on the Strip?
The STRAT Hotel Casino Tower is located at 2000 S Las Vegas Blvd, at the north end of the Las Vegas Strip near Sahara Avenue. It's about a 10–15 minute drive or rideshare from the center of the Strip. The STRAT is a full resort property with on-site parking, restaurants, and of course the observation deck and thrill rides on top of the tower if you want to make a full evening of it.
