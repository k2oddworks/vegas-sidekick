What is Brad Garrett's Comedy Club?

Brad Garrett’s Comedy Club is a 90-minute stand-up show at The District inside MGM Grand. It operates like a real comedy club rather than a fixed residency: the lineup rotates, so the comedians onstage can change from one visit or week to the next.

That rotating format is the reason to choose it. You are booking a club night built around multiple stand-up sets, not one permanent headliner performing the same production every evening. Brad Garrett’s name is on the room, but the broader draw is the changing mix of working comics.

Frequently asked questions

Does Brad Garrett perform every night?
No — Brad Garrett appears on select nights, not every performance. The club operates as a rotating comedy room, with a new lineup announced weekly that includes both established headliners and up-and-coming talent. Check the current schedule before you book to see who's on stage the night you're attending.
How does zone seating work?
Your ticket places you in a zone — Zone 1 (Kings Row), Zone 2 (main floor), or Zone 3 (outer). Specific seating within your zone is assigned when you arrive at the venue, based on best available at that time. The earlier you arrive, the more choices you'll have within your purchased zone. Your zone tier is locked at purchase.
What is the age requirement?
This show is strictly for guests 18 and older. No exceptions, no matter how mature they seem. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID — it will be checked at the door. Guests who cannot verify their age will not be admitted.
Where exactly is Brad Garrett's inside MGM Grand?
Brad Garrett's Comedy Club is located inside The District, the restaurant and entertainment area within MGM Grand at 3799 S Las Vegas Blvd. Follow signs for The District once you're inside the casino. The venue is clearly marked within that section of the property.
How long is the show?
The show runs approximately 90 minutes with no intermission. Doors open before the 8:00 PM showtime. Arriving 15–20 minutes early gives you time to get settled and get the best available seat within your zone.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Refunds are available for exceptional circumstances such as the unlikely event that a show cancels. Check the full terms and conditions before purchasing.
