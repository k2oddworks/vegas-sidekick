Activate’s first Nevada location is scheduled to open Saturday, October 3, 2026, at Town Square Las Vegas. If your group wants something more active than another bar, buffet or sit-down attraction, this is the part that matters: you’re physically inside the games.

Activate currently lists 14 game rooms at its Town Square location.

What is Activate?

Activate is an active-gaming venue built around small, technology-driven rooms. Instead of sitting at a screen, players jump, dodge, solve, climb and coordinate while the room reacts in real time. Games are short — Activate says individual challenges generally run about one to three minutes — so the experience is designed around trying different rooms, levels and strategies rather than committing to one long game.

The Town Square location is Activate’s first in Nevada. The company currently describes the Las Vegas venue as having 14 game rooms, with groups starting at two players and large events accommodating up to 80.

What games are inside?

The format mixes physical challenges with puzzle and strategy games. Early Las Vegas opening information specifically named Hoops, Laser, Mega Grid and Hide and Press. The bigger hook is replayability: rooms can contain multiple games and difficulty levels, so you can keep moving instead of waiting through one fixed attraction.

Activate’s system also uses electronic wristbands to track gameplay and achievements. That makes the competitive part useful for mixed groups — you can chase scores if you want to, but nobody needs to arrive knowing the rules.

Who is Activate Las Vegas best for?

Groups of friends

Good when you want something competitive before dinner, drinks or a night out.

Families & teens

Ages 6+ are welcome, and adjustable difficulty gives mixed-age groups some room to work.

Convention groups

Team play and group capacity up to 80 make this a practical off-site option.

There is a physical component. Activate recommends comfortable clothing and clean, flat, closed-toe shoes because some games involve running, jumping, crawling or climbing. A waiver is also required.

Activate Las Vegas FAQ

When does Activate Las Vegas open?

Saturday, October 3, 2026, according to Activate’s current Las Vegas Town Square page.

Where is Activate Las Vegas?

Town Square Las Vegas at 6611 S Las Vegas Blvd, Suite A143, Las Vegas, NV 89119.

How old do you have to be?

Activate lists the Town Square location for ages 6 and older. You choose difficulty levels inside the games.

How many game rooms are there?

Activate currently lists 14 game rooms for Las Vegas. Early opening material described 15 arenas; we’re using the operator’s current number.

Do I need a reservation?

Not necessarily. Activate says walk-ins are welcome when space is available, but recommends booking in advance.

What should I wear?

Wear clothes you can move in. Activate recommends clean, flat, closed-toe footwear because games can involve running, jumping, crawling and climbing.
