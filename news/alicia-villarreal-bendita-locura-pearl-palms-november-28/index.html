Alicia Villarreal’s Bendita Locura Tour lands at The Pearl Concert Theater at Palms on Saturday, November 28. Palms currently lists doors at 7 PM and the concert at 8 PM.

What matters now

The June presale and on-sale details have expired. If you’re planning around this show now, the useful information is the date, the 7 PM doors, the 8 PM start and the fact that this is a single Las Vegas stop at The Pearl.

Why this booking fits The Pearl

Villarreal’s catalog moves across norteño, mariachi, banda and pop, and The Pearl is a room where a vocal-first concert can still feel intimate without being small. The original announcement framed Bendita Locura as a career-spanning celebration rather than a one-album tour.

Build the night around Palms

Palms is west of the Strip, so this is easier as a Palms night than as something you squeeze between Strip plans. Give yourself enough time to arrive before doors if you want dinner or drinks at the resort first.

🌵 Kris’s take

This is a better booking than the generic “Latin concert in Vegas” label makes it sound. Villarreal has a catalog people actually grew up with, and The Pearl is small enough that the songs should do more of the work than the production.
