Donny Osmond Extends His Harrah's Residency Through 2027

by Kris Kidd · August 29, 2026

Marking five years in Harrah's Showroom, the Strip headliner is adding an AI-reimagined "Puppy Love" to a set that already spans six decades on stage.

Donny Osmond isn't going anywhere. The singer has extended his residency in Harrah's Showroom at Harrah's Las Vegas through June 2027, carrying the show past its fifth anniversary on the Strip and locking in another full year of the high-energy career retrospective that's become one of the more consistent tickets in town.

Osmond framed the extension as a milestone in its own right when the news broke:

"Between my time on stage at Flamingo and Harrah's, it's been 16 wonderful years performing on The Strip. Sixteen … still not quite old enough for the casinos, but it still feels like quite an accomplishment.

"I am grateful to the many fans who have sung along and danced with me over the years, and I look forward to continuing the fun in 2027. The Las Vegas community has been incredibly welcoming, and I am proud to call this city my second home."

— Donny Osmond

A 16-Year Count, With Deeper Roots Than That

The number Osmond is counting starts with the joint residency he and sister Marie opened at the Flamingo back in 2008, and runs through the five years since he moved into Harrah's Showroom on his own. But the Osmond name was part of the Las Vegas entertainment story long before that Flamingo marquee ever went up — a fact worth sitting with when a single-artist residency makes it to a fifth anniversary at all.

"Sixteen years across two Strip residencies is already rare air, but the Osmonds' history with this town goes back a lot further than the Flamingo marquee. Longevity like that doesn't happen by accident anymore — rooms get renovated, headliners rotate, audiences move on to whatever's new. A show that's still selling out five years in, and still getting a new segment added every single year, isn't coasting on nostalgia. It's one of the best-run headliner productions on the Strip, full stop."

— Kris Kidd, Vegas Sidekick Founder

What's New for the Anniversary Run

To mark year five, the production is leaning further into technology than it ever has. Using original multitrack recordings and film footage from the 1970s, the team built an AI-assisted version of "Puppy Love," Osmond's 1972 hit — pairing archival audio with a live-controlled visual performance that lets him re-create the moment on his own terms, more than 50 years later.

The set moves from a decades-spanning career retrospective to a Broadway-style production number in the same night.

It joins a set that already swings from a Broadway-style staging of "I'll Make a Man Out of You" — Osmond's own contribution to Disney's "Mulan" — to "Auto-rap-ography," a rap number set against photos and video pulled from his 60-year career. One segment hands the room the reins entirely: any song he's ever recorded is fair game for a request, no setlist required.

Five Years, Five Awards

The show has picked up Best Resident Performer/Headliner and Best Production Show at the Las Vegas Review-Journal's Best of Las Vegas Awards every year since it opened in 2021 — including both again in 2025, a streak that hasn't broken once.

Who's Behind It

The production is directed by Raj Kapoor, whose résumé includes the Oscars, the Grammys, the Emmys, and Las Vegas stagings for the Backstreet Boys, Shania Twain, and Mariah Carey. Choreography comes from NappyTabs, the Emmy-winning duo.
