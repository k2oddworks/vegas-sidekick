Caesars announced August 27 that Donny Osmond’s Harrah’s Showroom residency is extended through June 2027. The old ticket-on-sale clock is already obsolete; the useful part is that this is no longer a short extension.

The extension is the story now

Osmond’s run at Harrah’s has reached its fifth anniversary, giving visitors considerably more date flexibility than the original announcement cycle suggested.

What changed for the fifth anniversary

Caesars says the anniversary version adds an AI-reimagined “Puppy Love” segment and a new Auto-rap-ography section alongside the career-spanning material that anchors the residency.

🌵 Kris's take

A five-year Vegas run is useful because it tells you the show has found its audience. If Donny is on your list, you have real date flexibility now instead of needing to force one specific trip.
