Dolby Live is inside Park MGM.

Dolby Live is at 3770 S Las Vegas Blvd. If you're already staying around the south-central Strip, this is an easy concert to build a night around without turning transportation into the main event.

Park MGM says the box office opens at 2 PM on event days and remains open until 30 minutes after the scheduled showtime.

🌵 Sidekick take

If Marco Antonio Solís is the reason you're in Vegas that weekend, I'd pick the night that works better for the rest of your trip rather than overthinking Friday versus Saturday. Same room, same listed 8 PM start. Give yourself enough time to get into Park MGM before doors and make the concert the anchor of the night.
