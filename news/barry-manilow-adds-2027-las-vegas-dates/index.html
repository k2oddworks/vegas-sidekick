Barry Manilow's Westgate run now reaches into 2027

Newly posted Las Vegas performances give Manilow fans more chances to plan a Westgate night next year.

By Kris Kidd · September 15, 2026

Barry Manilow isn't wrapping up Las Vegas with 2026. His calendar at the International Theater at Westgate now includes 2027 performances, extending one of the city's most established headliner runs into another year.

Current ticket listings show Westgate dates in February and March 2027, followed by April 1, 2 and 3. Live Nation currently lists February 18–20, February 25–27 and March 25–27 as rescheduled performances, with the April dates also posted.

For Vegas visitors, the useful part is more flexibility

Manilow's residency has always worked differently from a nightly production show. He plays concentrated date blocks, so travelers usually have to build the trip around when he's actually in the room. Extending the calendar into 2027 gives fans a much bigger planning window instead of forcing everything into the remaining fall and holiday dates this year.

Westgate remains part of the story

The International Theater isn't just another room for this residency. It's the same Las Vegas venue lineage associated with Elvis Presley's historic run, and Manilow has built his own record-setting chapter there. For someone specifically coming to Vegas to see Barry Manilow, that pairing of performer and room is a meaningful part of the night.

🌵 Kris's take

Barry Manilow extending into 2027 makes sense because this is one of those Vegas shows where the performer and the room have become tied together. People aren't stumbling into this one because they had an empty Tuesday night. They're coming to Westgate to see Barry. More 2027 dates simply make it easier to build the Vegas trip around him.

Ticket timing

Ticketmaster currently shows the April 2027 performances with presales beginning September 15 and general sale scheduled for Friday, September 18 at 10 a.m. Pacific. Dates and availability can change, so check the current calendar before locking in travel.
