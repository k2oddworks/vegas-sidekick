This was a calendar-building story: one week in May produced Las Vegas dates across Sphere, MGM Grand Garden Arena, Dolby Live, House of Blues, Brooklyn Bowl, The Pearl and The Venetian.

The headline bookings

The original roundup led with Eagles adding Sphere dates, KATSEYE announcing MGM Grand Garden Arena for November 14, and Matt Rife booking Dolby Live for December 4. It also tracked Carlos Santana’s September House of Blues dates and a broader batch of club, theater and arena shows.

What aged out

The Friday-on-sale framing aged out almost immediately. So did language implying availability would disappear. Those details are gone because an archive should explain what was announced, not keep pretending it is May.

What still matters

The story is a useful snapshot of the range of rooms doing the work: not just Sphere and arenas, but Brooklyn Bowl, House of Blues, The Pearl and Palazzo Theatre.

🌵 Kris’s take

The takeaway wasn’t Eagles versus Matt Rife versus KATSEYE. It was the depth. When major rooms and smaller rooms all announce in the same week, Vegas starts looking like a functioning entertainment city instead of a handful of headliners.
