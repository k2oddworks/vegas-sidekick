Soda Stereo’s ECOS arrives at Dolby Live at Park MGM on Sunday, September 13. Park MGM lists doors at 7 PM and the event at 8 PM.

What matters now

The original announcement focused on the on-sale. With the concert now days away, that is old news. The useful part is the plan: one Sunday night at Dolby Live, with doors at 7 PM and the show at 8 PM.

Why this one is different

Soda Stereo is not a routine nostalgia booking. The band’s catalog has an unusually deep cultural footprint across Latin America, and ECOS is built around that history rather than pretending the past never happened.
