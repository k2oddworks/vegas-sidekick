Daughtry’s own tour calendar lists the November 7 Las Vegas stop as an unplugged show at Pearl Concert Theater, with Ryan Perdz on the bill.

The format matters more than the anniversary label

This is not the standard arena-rock version of a Daughtry set. The stripped-back setup changes what the Vegas date is for.

Who should pick it

If you want the big electric production, unplugged is the tradeoff. If you want the songs and Chris Daughtry’s voice pushed forward, it is the reason to book.

🌵 Kris's take

I would choose this specifically because it is unplugged, not in spite of it. A smaller Pearl room plus stripped-back arrangements gives the Vegas date a different job than a normal Daughtry tour stop.
