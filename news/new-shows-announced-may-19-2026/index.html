This May 19 roundup caught Marco Antonio Solís, Smashing Pumpkins, Tumua, for KING + COUNTRY and Clay Walker in one announcement wave.

The five bookings

Marco Antonio Solís brought Tour Gratitud to Dolby Live for September 11–12; Smashing Pumpkins announced MGM Grand Garden Arena for October 30; Tumua landed The Palazzo Theatre October 9; for KING + COUNTRY booked The Pearl October 8; and Clay Walker booked The Venetian Theatre December 5.

What changed since May

All five stories moved past announcement mode. The individual pages now carry the current dates, times and venue planning details instead of codes, countdowns and old on-sale instructions.
