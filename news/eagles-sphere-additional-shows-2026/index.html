Eagles return to Sphere on Friday, September 18 and Saturday, September 19. Since this story first ran in May, the residency has expanded twice more: December dates were added in July, January 2027 dates followed in August, and Sphere Entertainment now puts the full run at 72 shows.

What changed since the original announcement

The original story covered six added dates and a 64-show total. That is no longer the current picture. The November date listed as November 12 was also wrong; the band’s current schedule lists Friday, November 13.

The old presale and general on-sale clocks are gone from this page because they have expired. The Eagles’ current official announcement lists tickets from $175 and says the displayed price includes taxes and fees. Actual inventory and available prices can move by performance.

Why Sphere still makes sense for this catalog

The original reporting got the core fit right: this is a catalog built around big visual shorthand — highways, deserts, Hotel California, decades of American-road iconography — in a room designed to wrap imagery around the audience. Sphere is not rescuing the songs; it is giving a familiar catalog a different scale.

🌵 Kris's take
The headline is not “six more shows” anymore. It’s that the Eagles have turned Sphere into a long-form Vegas residency instead of a limited engagement. If you’re choosing between dates, I’d pick the weekend that works for your trip and stop chasing old presale windows — there are enough performances on the calendar now that the date matters more than the announcement cycle.
— Kris Kidd, Vegas Sidekick
