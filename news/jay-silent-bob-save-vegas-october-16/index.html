What to knowKris's takeFAQ

The Venetian currently lists Jay & Silent Bob Save Vegas for Friday, October 16 at 9:30 PM.

This is not a movie screening

The Venetian describes the show as Kevin Smith and Jason Mewes dropping the scripts and telling stories about movies, family and the three decades they have spent as Jay and Silent Bob.

Build the night around the late start

At 9:30 PM, this works well after dinner. It is a bad fit if you want an early night.

🌵 Kris's take

The tradeoff is also the filter: this is very specifically for people who enjoy Kevin Smith and Jason Mewes talking as themselves. If that sentence already sounds fun, the concept does not need much more selling.
