How the ticket registration works

For the U.S. dates, registration is the public route into the ticket sale. Fans need to register at oasisinet.com by Thursday, September 17 at 8 a.m. PT.

If your registration is successful, you’ll receive a unique, non-transferable code for an allocated onsale window. The U.S. onsale windows are scheduled for Friday, September 25, with the exact time tied to the window you’re assigned.

Useful part: there is no separate general onsale or other public presale announced for these U.S. dates. Registering does not guarantee tickets, but skipping registration means you won’t have access to the public sale process that has been announced.

What tickets cost

The announced standard ticket range for the U.S. dates is $122.65 to $541.05, including fees and taxes. That gives you a real range before registration instead of a mystery starting point.

Why this is a big Vegas booking

From a ticket-industry perspective, the interesting part is not just that Oasis are playing Vegas. It’s the scale: three stadium nights, a reunion people have waited years to see, and dates announced far enough ahead that fans can build an entire trip around them.

That changes how people shop Vegas. Instead of picking a weekend and then asking what’s playing, these shows reverse the order: you get the concert date first, then the flight, hotel and everything else.
