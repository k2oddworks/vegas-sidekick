Three nights at Allegiant Stadium is the story

Vegas gets major concert announcements constantly. Three stadium dates is different. Oasis isn’t dropping in for one routing stop; the band is giving Las Vegas a three-night run on August 20, 21 and 24.

Richard Ashcroft is the announced special guest for all three nights.

🌵 Kris’s take

Oasis coming to Las Vegas is one of those announcements that immediately feels bigger than another concert on the calendar. Three nights at Allegiant Stadium tells you the scale of it. Vegas gets plenty of huge names, but getting Oasis here, together, is genuinely exciting. This is going to be one of the shows people build an entire Vegas trip around.

How the ticket registration works

Fans need to register through Oasis before Thursday, September 17 at 8 a.m. PT. Successful registrants can receive a unique, non-transferable code for an allocated U.S. onsale window.

The announced U.S. onsale windows begin Friday, September 25. Registration does not guarantee a code or tickets. At the moment, the registration process is the public route that has been announced for these shows.

The announced ticket range

Standard tickets were announced at $122.65–$541.05, including fees and taxes. That is the published range, not a promise that every price point will still be available when your onsale window opens.
