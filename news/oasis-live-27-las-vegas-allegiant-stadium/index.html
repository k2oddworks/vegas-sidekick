Oasis Announce Three Las Vegas Shows at Allegiant Stadium in 2027

August 20, 21 and 24 at Allegiant Stadium, with Richard Ashcroft opening. The ticket process is registration-first, and the deadline is September 17 at 8 a.m. PT.

Oasis are coming to Las Vegas for three nights at Allegiant Stadium in August 2027. That’s the headline. The part that matters if you want tickets: this one does not have a normal public onsale.

Oasis Live ’27 lands in Las Vegas on Friday, August 20, Saturday, August 21 and Tuesday, August 24, 2027. Richard Ashcroft is the announced special guest for all three shows.

Three stadium dates is the important number here. Vegas gets major concert stops every week, but a three-night run at Allegiant is destination-scale. These are the kinds of dates that become the reason for the trip instead of something you add after the hotel is booked.

How the ticket registration works

For the U.S. dates, registration is the public route into the ticket sale. Fans need to register at oasisinet.com by Thursday, September 17 at 8 a.m. PT.

If your registration is successful, you’ll receive a unique, non-transferable code for an allocated onsale window. The U.S. onsale windows are scheduled for Friday, September 25.

Useful part: there is no separate general onsale or other public presale announced for these U.S. dates.
