Spike's take

Booking one show for a mixed-age crew? Blue Man Group, every time — nobody's too old or too young for it. Traveling with little ones? Make it a matinee: Popovich's rescue pets or Mac King. And if the kids are wired for chaos, BattleBots will make you the coolest parent on the trip.

Family Shows — FAQ

What's the best Vegas show for families?

Blue Man Group at the Luxor is the definitive family show in Las Vegas — no words, no scary content, just music, color, and comedy that lands for a 7-year-old and a grandparent equally. Mystère by Cirque du Soleil is the runner-up.

What are the best Vegas shows for young kids?

Afternoon matinees are ideal for younger kids: Mac King Comedy Magic, Popovich Comedy Pet Theater (rescue dogs and cats doing tricks), and Nathan Burton Comedy Magic ($32) are all daytime, silly, and short enough for shorter attention spans.

What's a good family show that isn't too expensive?

Nathan Burton Comedy Magic ($32), Popovich Comedy Pet Theater ($34), The Australian Bee Gees ($36), and V – The Ultimate Variety Show ($46) are all genuinely fun all-ages shows for under $50 a ticket.

Which Cirque du Soleil show is best for kids?

Mystère at Treasure Island is the most kid-friendly Cirque du Soleil show — whimsical, funny, and all-ages. KÀ (a martial-arts epic) and Michael Jackson ONE (a high-energy music celebration) are great for older kids and teens.

Are there dinner shows the whole family can enjoy?

Tournament of Kings at Excalibur is the classic family dinner show — a medieval jousting tournament where you eat a whole roasted-chicken feast with your hands and cheer for your knight. Kids love it.
