Cheap Vegas Shows — FAQ

What's the cheapest show in Las Vegas?

The cheapest shows on the Strip start at $27. Jimmy Kimmel's Comedy Club takes the lowest ticket on this list at $27, with Marc Savard Comedy Hypnosis just behind at $28.

Are cheap Vegas shows actually any good?

Yes. Price in Vegas is mostly about venue size and production budget, not quality. A $30 magic or comedy show in an intimate theater can be every bit as entertaining as a $150 arena spectacle — it's just closer and smaller. Every show on this list is one we'd send our own friends to.

What's the cheapest magic show in Vegas?

Allstars of Magic and Paranormal are tied for the cheapest magic show at $31, followed by Nathan Burton Comedy Magic at $32. For families, Nathan Burton, Paranormal, and Mac King are the easy picks.

Can you see a Vegas show for under $30?

Yes — Jimmy Kimmel's Comedy Club ($27) and Marc Savard Comedy Hypnosis ($28) both come in under thirty dollars, with Allstars of Magic and Paranormal ($31) just above.

Why do so many shows on this list share the same venue?

Seven of the $40 shows here — All Motown, Sinatra Live!, Queen Selena, Sir Elton, King of Diamonds, America The Show, and Wastin' Away — all run at the Athena Showlounge inside Alexis Park Resort, an off-Strip venue that rotates a roster of tribute and variety acts through one intimate room. Same price, same room, a different show every night of the week.
