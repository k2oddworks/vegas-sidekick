Vegas Tribute Shows — FAQ

What's the best tribute show in Vegas?

MJ Live is our top pick overall — a full-production Michael Jackson concert with a live band and choreography, nightly at Saxe Theater inside Planet Hollywood. If you want scale and variety in one night, Ikons of Rock covers a dozen classic-rock acts on one stage at The STRAT.

What's the cheapest tribute show in Vegas?

The Australian Bee Gees Show at $36 is the cheapest standalone tribute. The seven shows in the Alexis Park tribute room start at $40, with All Shook Up at $47.

What is the Alexis Park tribute room?

The Athena Showlounge at Alexis Park Resort, just east of the Strip, runs seven different tribute shows across the week — Sinatra, Elvis, Motown, Elton John, Selena, Neil Diamond, and Jimmy Buffett — all in the same intimate room, all starting at $40.

What's the best Elvis tribute show in Vegas?

The King Comes Home at Westgate Las Vegas is the fuller production at 90 minutes with powerhouse vocals. All Shook Up at Alexis Park is the more casual, lower-priced alternative.

Is there a Michael Jackson tribute show in Vegas?

Yes — MJ Live at Saxe Theater inside Planet Hollywood is a dedicated Michael Jackson tribute concert. (Michael Jackson ONE by Cirque du Soleil at Mandalay Bay is a different kind of show — an original acrobatic production built around his music, not a tribute concert — and it's ranked separately in our Best Cirque Shows guide.)
