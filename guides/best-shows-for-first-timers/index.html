First-Timer Shows — FAQ

What show should I see on my first trip to Vegas?

If you want the single most iconic Vegas experience, it's O by Cirque du Soleil at the Bellagio — the aquatic masterpiece that defines a first trip. For the quintessential modern Vegas night out (adults only), it's Absinthe at Caesars Palace.

What's the most iconic Cirque du Soleil show in Vegas?

O at the Bellagio is the most iconic — performed in and above a 1.5-million-gallon pool. KÀ (a martial-arts epic) and Michael Jackson ONE (a music celebration) are the other must-sees, and Mystère is the best-value first Cirque.

What's a good first Vegas show that isn't too expensive?

Mat Franco, VEGAS! The Show, KÀ and Mystère are strong first-trip options when you want a lower price than the biggest Strip splurges. Check your date because starting prices move.

Is Absinthe a good show for first-timers?

Yes — for adults, Absinthe is arguably the definitive modern Vegas show: world-class acrobatics inches from your seat wrapped in raunchy, hilarious comedy. It's 18+ and not for kids, but it's a first-timer favorite for grown-ups.

How many shows should I see on my first Vegas trip?

Most first-timers see one to three shows across a long weekend — typically one big splurge spectacle (like O or Michael Jackson ONE) plus one or two lower-key picks like a magic show, comedy show or classic Vegas revue.
