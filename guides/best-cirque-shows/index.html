Vegas Cirque Shows — FAQ

What is the best Cirque du Soleil show in Las Vegas?

"O" at the Bellagio is the consensus best — the aquatic masterpiece performed in, on and above a 1.5-million-gallon pool. It's the most technically ambitious and the one to see if you only see one. Michael Jackson ONE is the best pick if you want music and energy over pure artistry.

How many Cirque du Soleil shows are in Las Vegas?

There are four current resident Cirque du Soleil shows in Las Vegas right now: "O" at Bellagio, Michael Jackson ONE at Mandalay Bay, KÀ at MGM Grand, Mystère at Treasure Island.

Which Cirque show is best for families?

Mystère at Treasure Island — it's the original Vegas Cirque show, the most all-ages friendly, and usually the best value of the classics. KÀ is also a strong pick for older kids who like an action, martial-arts feel.

What is the cheapest Cirque du Soleil show in Vegas?

Mystère at TI is usually the lowest-priced current resident Cirque option, starting around $84. Check your date because prices move by performance and seat.

"O" vs Michael Jackson ONE — which should I see?

See "O" for artistry and spectacle — it's the once-in-a-lifetime water show. See Michael Jackson ONE for energy, hits and a crowd on its feet. "O" is the more impressive production; Michael Jackson ONE is the more fun night out. If it's your first Cirque and you love his music, ONE is the easy call.
