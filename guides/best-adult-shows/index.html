Vegas Adult Shows — FAQ

What's the best adult show in Vegas?

Magic Mike Live is our top overall pick — the biggest production budget of any male revue on the Strip, created and directed by Channing Tatum. X Burlesque is the classic pick if you want the original, award-winning topless revue instead.

What's the cheapest adult show in Vegas?

The Empire Strips Back at $35, followed by Fantasy at $40 — both genuinely good, not just cheap.

What's the best male revue in Vegas?

Magic Mike Live has the biggest production budget, Chippendales is the most iconic name in the category worldwide, and Thunder From Down Under is the long-running bachelorette-party favorite. All three are worth it for different kinds of nights out.

What's the best female revue in Vegas?

X Burlesque is the original and still the standard-bearer. Rouge is the more intimate alternative at The STRAT, and X Rocks and X Country put a rock or country spin on the same format.

How explicit are Vegas adult shows?

All of these are 18+ (Zombie Burlesque is the one exception at 16+) and feature toplessness, but none of them are strip clubs — they're choreographed theatrical productions, not clubs. Expect stage nudity and adult themes, not audience-participation extremes.

What age do I need to be for adult shows in Vegas?

Most require guests to be 18 or older with valid photo ID. Zombie Burlesque is the one exception on this list at 16+. Every guest needs a ticket regardless of age.
