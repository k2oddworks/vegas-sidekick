Vegas Sidekick publishes Sidekick Index factual datasets so people can cite them, analyze them, build on them and share them instead of starting from zero.

Sidekick Index factual datasets that are identified as open data are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0).

In plain English

You can copy, share, analyze, adapt and commercially reuse the licensed Sidekick Index data. Give Vegas Sidekick credit, link back to the dataset you used, link to CC BY 4.0, and say if you changed the data.

What you can do

  • Quote or cite the data in articles, newsletters, videos and research.
  • Create charts, analysis, apps or other derivative work from the licensed data.
  • Share or republish the licensed dataset.
  • Use the licensed data commercially, as CC BY 4.0 allows.

What is not included

  • Vegas Sidekick editorial writing, show descriptions or recommendations.
  • The Vegas Sidekick name, logos, Spike mascot or other trademarks.
  • Photography, graphics, page layouts or site design.
  • Third-party source material, trademarks or content that Vegas Sidekick does not own.

Attribution

A simple credit is enough. Name Vegas Sidekick, link to the specific Sidekick Index page or dataset you used, link to the CC BY 4.0 license, and note material changes you made.
