How I rank these.

I care about whether the show feels worth one of a first-timer's limited Vegas nights: production value, how broadly it lands, what makes it specifically Vegas, price, and the downside I'd tell a friend before they bought.

First-trip questions.

What show should I see on my first trip to Vegas?

If you want the classic big-ticket answer, “O.” If you're an adults-only group that wants something less polished and much dirtier, Absinthe.

Do I need to see more than one show?

No. One show you actually want beats stacking the itinerary. If you're doing two, I'd usually make them different: one spectacle and one comedy, magic or music show.

Should I just pick the cheapest show?

No. Price matters, but a cheap ticket to the wrong show is still money wasted. Start with fit, then compare price.
