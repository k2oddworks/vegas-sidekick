Some comics chase fame. Don Barnhart has spent his career chasing laughs. A Las Vegas headliner, touring comedian, hypnotist, producer and author, Don has entertained audiences at comedy clubs, casinos, cruise ships, and military bases around the world.

His approach combines sharp observation, improvisation and the confidence that comes from spending more than three decades onstage.
