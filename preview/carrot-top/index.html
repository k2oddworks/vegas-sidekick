The Show

Two decades on the same Strip stage — and never the same show twice

Scott "Carrot Top" Thompson has been making Las Vegas lose its mind since 2005, and the Atrium Showroom at the Luxor has been his home the entire time.

That makes it one of the longest-running comedy residencies in Las Vegas Strip history — and it's a title the show earns nightly rather than coasting on. The format is deceptively simple: trunk after trunk of custom-built props, delivered at a pace that never lets the room catch its breath. One bit rolls into the next before you've finished laughing at the last one. He's fast, physical and self-aware, and he doesn't need a long setup because the prop is the punchline.

The material turns over constantly. If you saw him five years ago, you have not seen this show. If you've never watched prop comedy performed at this level and this speed, this is the one to start with — and at 75 minutes with no intermission, it's an easy anchor for a night on the south end of the Strip.

The Atrium Showroom is small enough that there genuinely isn't a bad seat in the house. Every ticket puts you inside the room rather than watching from a distance — unusual for a residency this established.

Before you book

What to expect

🧰 Props that keep changing
Mostly new material every run, with a handful of crowd favourites audiences won't let him retire. Two visits a few years apart are two different shows.
⚡ Zero intermission
75 minutes, no break, no filler. It moves at the speed of somebody with something to prove — you won't be checking the time.
🎪 A genuinely small room
The Atrium Showroom is intimate enough that even the back row feels close. This is not an arena show, and it isn't trying to be.
🎭 The front rows get worked
Expect eye contact, prop debris and full unfiltered attention if you sit up front. The most immersive seats in the house — and the most exposed.
🎤 Optional meet & greet
A separate add-on at 6:55 PM with personal time and a photo. It sells out independently of show tickets, so grab it at checkout or not at all.
🍸 Drinks at your seat
Bar service runs before the show. Get one in hand during seating rather than trying to flag anyone once the first trunk is open.
