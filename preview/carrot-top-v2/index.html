Why this show, still

The longest-running comedy residency on the Strip didn't get there by coasting

Scott "Carrot Top" Thompson opened at the Luxor in 2005. He's still there — same Atrium Showroom, new props every run.

That's the part people miss: the format sounds simple on paper — comedian opens trunks, pulls out gadgets, builds a joke around each one — but the material genuinely turns over. Regulars will tell you the show they saw last year isn't the show running this month. The pace is the other half of it: one bit rolls into the next before the laugh from the last one has finished, so 75 minutes moves like 40.

The room helps. The Atrium Showroom is small by Strip standards, which means there's no seat that's actually far from the stage — just seats that are closer to the chaos than others.
