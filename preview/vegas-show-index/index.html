03 · The method

Less persuasion. More record keeping.

The concept is intentionally closer to a reference publication than a personality-driven travel site.

01

Lead with useful numbers.

Starting price, runtime, schedule and age restrictions sit near the top instead of being buried under a sales pitch.

02

Separate facts from judgment.

Venue, schedule and restrictions are factual records. Seating guidance and comparisons are clearly presented as guidance.

03

Keep the record current.

Every show profile carries a visible information-confirmed date so a visitor knows when the page was last reviewed.
