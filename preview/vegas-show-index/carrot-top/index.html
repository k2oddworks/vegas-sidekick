01 · Show profile

What happens onstage

Carrot Top’s Las Vegas show is prop comedy at high speed: physical sight gags, handmade inventions, topical references and short-form jokes that move on quickly instead of settling into long stand-up stories.

The format is deliberately visual. A joke may start with an oversized prop, a modified household object or a ridiculous costume, then disappear almost as quickly as it arrived. That constant turnover is the show’s defining feature and a big reason the 75-minute runtime feels compact.

Carrot Top performs in the Atrium Showroom at Luxor. The regular schedule is Monday through Saturday at 8 PM, with Sunday dark. The show is restricted to guests age 16 and older; guests 18 and under must be accompanied by an adult age 21 or older.
