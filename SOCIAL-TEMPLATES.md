# Vegas Sidekick — 2027 Social Template System

Phase 4 production templates. Governed by BRAND.md, SPIKE-CHARACTER-BIBLE.md and GOOD-TO-KNOW.md. The editable browser studio is [docs/social-template-studio.html](docs/social-template-studio.html). It is an unlisted, noindex static design tool, not a private/authenticated service. It does not publish posts or store uploaded photos.

| Series | Default format | Treatment | Required copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Good to Know | 1080 × 1080 | Dark base, lime/amber, full-width rows | Exact existing header/subtitle, show, change, verified detail |
| Just Announced | 1080 × 1350 | Cyan headline band, navy body | Show/act, announcement detail; date/venue if useful |
| Spike's Pick | 1080 × 1350 | Lime headline band, navy body | Actual recommendation and specific reason |
| Vegas has a show for that | 1080 × 1080 | Magenta headline band, navy body | Visitor situation and actual pick |
| Video end card | 1080 × 1920 | Cyan, official avatar, pill, slogan, domain | Fixed brand message |

Other canvas ratios are available for non-Good-to-Know designs: square, portrait, vertical and landscape. These are working layout defaults, not platform guarantees. End card is a static PNG for use in video editing, not an animated video. Keep important content away from platform controls and check the actual preview before publishing.

## Workflow
1. Select a series and size. Enter verified facts; do not publish placeholders.
2. Use approved artwork from the repository or supplied by the rights holder. The photo picker is optional for applicable designs; Good to Know currently supports clean text-only rows. Existing photo-led Good to Know editions remain valid.
3. Keep headlines short, supporting copy useful and exact show names intact.
4. Inspect the preview, confirm the review checkbox, then save the PNG. Publish manually.
5. Store final approved Good to Know graphics in /images/good-to-know/; other final campaign assets in a purpose-specific brand subdirectory when needed.

The studio uses the actual transparent pill and official Spike avatar, not redrawn artwork. Fonts are Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter with browser fallbacks. No claims about prices, schedules or availability are autogenerated. Export checks required fields and length, not the truth of the facts. Historical posts and website layouts remain unchanged.

## Review
Check on phone and desktop; confirm font loading, logo rendering, readable text at thumbnail size, image crop, and PNG export for each ratio. Video end cards need a separate duration/platform safe-area review. The proposed design treatments are initial production templates; revise after visual testing rather than proliferating variants.
