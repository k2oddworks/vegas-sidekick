The Season Queens vibe

Not fancy.
Just serious food.

This design leans into the flyer’s warm orange, cream, black and gold palette, but cleans it up for the web. The food stays front and center, while the typography feels loud enough to stop a scroll without turning the site into a luxury restaurant.

“Dinner should look good before you even open the carry-out box.”

Motion is used where it helps: floating food photography in the hero, reveal animations as you scroll, subtle card tilt on desktop, a moving carry-out marquee and a little celebratory burst when someone tests the cart.
