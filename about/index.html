The Mission

Maximize your night, not your bill.

Las Vegas sells dreams. Too often, it sells them at the cost of your wallet, your time, and your trust. Tourist traps. Hidden fees. Manufactured hype. Peak-season chaos disguised as “must-do” experiences.

Vegas Sidekick exists to change that. We live here. We know what tourists actually love — and what’s designed to squeeze every dollar out of you. We’re not here to maximize your spending — we’re here to maximize your experience. That means honest recommendations. Real prices. Inside knowledge without the Vegas theater.

We show you where the real magic happens. We tell you which shows deliver genuine value and which are engineered for the Strip. We respect your intelligence, your time, and your willingness to have fun without getting fleeced.
