About Kris Kidd

The story behind the picks.

Most Vegas show guides are built by algorithms, SEO farms, and people who’ve never actually sat in the theater. Vegas Sidekick is different — because I book these shows for a living, and I actually live here.

My name is Kris Kidd. I’ve been a Las Vegas local since 2005, and I’ve worked in this city’s ticketing and concierge world since 2006 — fifteen-plus years spent matching visitors to the right show, most recently as a high-end concierge at Hilton Grand Vacation Club. I’ve seen well over a hundred productions on and off the Strip: the resident shows that run every week, plus a handful of the big concerts that roll through each year.

I built Vegas Sidekick because I got tired of being told what to recommend. For years, my job was to point guests at whatever paid the house the most — not what was right for the person standing in front of me. That’s backwards. A great recommendation starts with who you are and what you want to spend, not with somebody’s commission. I’ve watched too many people pay too much for a show they weren’t even interested in. This site is my fix for that.

Spike — our deadpan saguaro sidekick — is that same straight talk with sunglasses on. No fake urgency, no hidden fees, no manufactured hype. Just honest picks from someone who’s actually been in the room, walked guests to their seats, and knows which sections are worth the money.

Want to know where to start? My personal favorites are VEGAS! The Show, Atomic Saloon Show, and Mac King — three completely different nights out, all worth every dollar. Vegas Sidekick isn’t a booking engine. It’s a point of view. After twenty years in this city, I’ve earned mine.
