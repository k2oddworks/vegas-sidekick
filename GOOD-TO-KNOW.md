# Vegas Sidekick — Good to Know Social Series

**Status:** Active recurring customer-facing social series  
**Introduced:** October 2026  
**Asset folder:** `/images/good-to-know/`

---

## What Good to Know is

**Good to Know** is Vegas Sidekick’s customer-service social layer for useful show changes a visitor may have missed.

It is not a breaking-news product and should not read like entertainment-industry news. Vegas Sidekick uses **Vegas Dispatch** for editorial/news coverage.

The distinction is simple:

- **Vegas Dispatch** = news, reporting, context, editorial.
- **Good to Know** = practical show-planning changes that affect someone choosing, booking or attending a Vegas show.

Good to Know should answer: **What changed, and what does the customer need to know now?**

---

## What belongs in Good to Know

Use the series when a verified change materially affects a customer planning to see a show, including:

- a show changes its start time
- a show moves to another theater or hotel
- a temporarily suspended show announces a return date
- regular performance days change
- a residency adds meaningful new dates
- a show announces a closing date
- a venue or production makes another verified change that materially affects planning or booking

Do not use Good to Know for generic entertainment news, rumors, minor promotional announcements, celebrity gossip or changes that do not affect the customer’s planning decision.

---

## Voice

Write like a knowledgeable Vegas local who works in tickets texting a friend.

Be:

- direct
- useful
- concise
- matter-of-fact
- friendly without hype
- specific with dates, times and venues

Do not use:

- fake urgency
- **Breaking** / **Breaking News**
- manufactured excitement
- PR language
- generic tourism copy
- unsupported claims
- rumors
- **selling fast** unless independently verified
- unnecessary editorial commentary

---

## Standard series header

Every graphic uses:

**GOOD TO KNOW**

**Show changes you may have missed.**

Do not substitute another recurring slogan without Kris explicitly changing the series identity.

---

## Visual system

Default format:

- square 1:1 social graphic
- target canvas: **1080 × 1080**
- dark / black base
- Vegas Sidekick lime and Warm Amber / orange accents
- high contrast
- readable on a phone
- clean and modern
- customer-facing, not tabloid
- enough negative space to avoid looking crowded

When multiple updates appear in one graphic, organize them as clean full-width horizontal rows. Each row should communicate immediately:

1. the show
2. what changed
3. the important time, date or venue

Make the changed fact visually dominant.

Vegas Sidekick branding belongs at the bottom with comfortable breathing room.

---

## Approved asset rule

When approved show photography, show artwork, logos or Vegas Sidekick branding assets already exist, **use those exact assets**.

Treat supplied or repo-approved artwork as source material to place, crop, scale, mask or overlay — not as inspiration for an AI-generated replacement.

Allowed:

- cropping
- scaling
- masking
- gradients
- subtle readability overlays
- non-destructive composition

Do not:

- generate replacement performers
- alter faces
- create lookalike performers
- invent costumes
- generate substitute show photography
- redraw or recreate a show logo
- redesign the Vegas Sidekick logo
- replace approved assets with AI approximations

The approved pixels must remain visibly recognizable in the finished composition.

For current show hero photography, use the approved hero asset referenced by the current show page unless Kris specifies another approved image.

Official Vegas Sidekick horizontal logo:

`/images/brand/logo-horizontal.png`

Use the actual transparent PNG. Do not recreate the wordmark, typography or cactus.

---

## Do not add

Unless Kris explicitly asks for it, Good to Know graphics should not include:

- extra slogans
- ticket prices
- ticket CTAs
- fake urgency
- **Different Times. New Details.**
- **Still a Great Vegas Lineup**
- **Breaking**
- **Breaking News**

Useful information is the product. Do not turn the card into an ad.

---

## File organization

All recurring Good to Know social assets belong in:

`/images/good-to-know/`

Use descriptive, date-stamped filenames when practical:

`good-to-know-YYYY-MM-DD.ext`

Do not scatter new Good to Know assets into the root `/images/` directory.
