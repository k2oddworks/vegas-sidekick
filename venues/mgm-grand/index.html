The rundown

The lion and the skyline, one intersection

MGM Grand is one of the largest resorts in the world — the giant bronze lion out front is the tell — and directly across Tropicana Avenue sits its neighbor New York-New York, with the Manhattan skyline and the roller coaster wrapped around it. An elevated pedestrian bridge connects the two, which is exactly why it's worth looking at them together.

The headliners are the reason most people come: KÀ by Cirque du Soleil and the Jabbawockeez at MGM Grand, and a second Cirque show — Mad Apple — just across the street at New York-New York. Around them, Now You See Me LIVE brings the heist-movie magic to the stage, and MGM Grand's comedy lineup (Brad Garrett's, Tape Face and Steve Falcon's) keeps the ticket prices friendly. Two hotels, one corner, a full week of nights out.

MGM Grand & New York-New York shows — FAQ

Together the two hotels host 7 shows we track — headlined by KÀ by Cirque du Soleil and the Jabbawockeez at MGM Grand, and Mad Apple by Cirque du Soleil across the street at New York-New York. Around them are Now You See Me LIVE and a strong comedy lineup (Brad Garrett's Comedy Club, Tape Face and Steve Falcon's). Tickets run about $44 to $80.

Yes — they sit on opposite corners of the Tropicana and Las Vegas Blvd intersection, connected by an elevated pedestrian bridge. It's a two-minute walk, so it's easy to pair a show at one with dinner or the roller coaster at the other.

Brad Garrett's Comedy Club at MGM Grand is usually the lowest at around $44, followed by Mad Apple at New York-New York near $56 and Tape Face in the high $50s.

KÀ is one of the most technically ambitious shows in Las Vegas — a Cirque du Soleil production staged on a massive rotating vertical platform, in a theater built specifically for it. It's the marquee reason to catch a show at MGM Grand.

Mad Apple is Cirque du Soleil's New York-themed variety show — a high-energy mix of acrobatics, magic, comedy and live music with a rooftop-party feel. It plays the New York-New York Theater right across the street from MGM Grand.
