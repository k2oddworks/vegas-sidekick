The rundown

The lion at the south end of the Strip

MGM Grand is one of the largest resorts in the world — the giant bronze lion out front is the tell — and it runs a full spread of entertainment to match. There's a Cirque du Soleil production staged in a theater built specifically for it, a long-running dance-crew residency, a big-illusion magic show, and an intimate comedy underground, all under one enormous roof.

The headliners are the reason most people come: KÀ by Cirque du Soleil in the KÀ Theatre and the Jabbawockeez in their own room. Around them, Now You See Me LIVE brings the heist-movie magic to the stage, and the comedy lineup — Brad Garrett's Comedy Club, Tape Face and Steve Falcon's — keeps the ticket prices friendly. It's a south-Strip anchor with something for every kind of night.
