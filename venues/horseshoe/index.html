The rundown

One showroom doing double duty, plus a room of its own

Horseshoe Las Vegas sits right on the center Strip, connected to Paris Las Vegas — most locals still know it by its old name, Bally's, and the resort makes no secret of the rebrand. It runs a full casino floor and two showrooms, though one of them hosts two very different shows.

The Laugh Factory is the venue's busiest room — home to the Laugh Factory comedy club's own nightly stand-up lineup, and also where mentalist Frederic Da Silva performs Paranormal on separate afternoons. They're not a package deal; check each show's own page for its specific days. X Rocks Theatre is a dedicated room built just for X Rocks, a topless rock revue. Tickets start at $31.
