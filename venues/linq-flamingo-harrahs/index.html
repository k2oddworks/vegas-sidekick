The rundown

Three hotels, one walkable corner

Flamingo, The LINQ and Harrah's sit right next to each other on the east side of the center Strip, stitched together by the open-air LINQ Promenade and the High Roller wheel. You can wander all three in a few minutes without crossing a street — which is exactly why it's worth looking at them together.

Between them they stack up 10 shows, from a $27 comedy club to genuine Vegas legends. Flamingo and Harrah's hold the legacy headliners — Wayne Newton and Donny Osmond — plus RuPaul's Drag Race LIVE!, while The LINQ is the magic-and-comedy heart of the block: Mat Franco, Jimmy Kimmel's Comedy Club and a pair of intimate magic rooms. It's one of the most show-dense, and most walkable, corners in town.
