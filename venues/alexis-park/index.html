The rundown

Nine shows, one room, $40 a ticket

Alexis Park Resort sits on Harmon Avenue just east of the Strip — directly across from Virgin Hotels and a few minutes from the airport. It is a non-gaming resort, which means no casino floor between the front door and your seat, and free parking when you get there.

Its Athena Showlounge runs the largest tribute lineup of any single room in Las Vegas — nine shows across the week, from Sinatra and Elton John to Selena, Neil Diamond and Jimmy Buffett, plus a Sunday Motown brunch. Showtimes run from 10 AM to 9 PM and barely overlap, so on most days you have a choice. Nearly everything is $40.

Good to know

Planning your night

📍

Where it is

375 E Harmon Ave — east of the Strip, directly across from Virgin Hotels and minutes from Harry Reid airport. A short rideshare from anywhere on the Strip, and free parking on site.

🎭

The room

Every show plays the Athena Showlounge — a small seated lounge with tables, side booths, reserved front blocks and general admission at the back. The tables are the Sweet Spot.

💸

Best value

Seven of the nine shows are $40. All Shook Up is $47 and Motown Brunch is $65 with food included — the cheapest cluster of full shows anywhere in Las Vegas.
