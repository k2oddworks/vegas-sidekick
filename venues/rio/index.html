The rundown

Off the Strip, on for value

The Rio sits just west of the Strip — about a mile off the main drag on Flamingo Road — and that's exactly why it's a value play: the shows are strong and the crowds are thinner. The anchor is a Las Vegas institution: Penn & Teller have headlined the Rio for decades, one of the longest-running residencies in town.

Around them, the lineup is refreshingly eclectic: the sci-fi burlesque parody The Empire Strips Back, the WOW – The Vegas Spectacular production, and a Las Vegas outpost of New York's famous Comedy Cellar. It's a quick rideshare from the Strip, and the trade-off is easier parking and a little more elbow room.

Planning your night

Where it is
