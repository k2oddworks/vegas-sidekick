The rundown

Three shows, two showrooms, one legendary tower

The STRAT sits at the far north end of the Las Vegas Strip, anchored by its 1,149-foot observation tower — the tallest freestanding tower in the United States, with thrill rides more than 100 stories up. Unlike some of the off-Strip showroom venues we cover, The STRAT runs a full casino floor, so this is very much a Strip resort — just a few minutes' drive from the center-Strip crowds.

The STRAT Theater hosts two very different headliners — Rouge, an intimate topless production, and Ikons of Rock, a classic-rock tribute spectacular — on their own nights. The separate Dragon Room runs LA Comedy Club's stand-up lineup. Tickets start at $35.
