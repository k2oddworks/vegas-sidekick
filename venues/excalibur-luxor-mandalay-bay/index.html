The rundown

Three hotels, one free tram

Excalibur, Luxor and Mandalay Bay anchor the far south end of the Strip, linked by a free tram that runs the length of the trio. Park once and you can hop between a medieval castle, a black-glass pyramid and a gold-glass resort without ever getting back in the car — which is exactly why it's worth looking at them together.

Between them they stack up 8 shows, from a $36 tribute act to one of the best Cirque productions in town. Mandalay Bay holds the marquee title — Michael Jackson ONE by Cirque du Soleil — while Luxor brings Blue Man Group and Carrot Top, and Excalibur is the value engine of the block with Tournament of Kings, Mac King and a pair of late-night crowd-pleasers. It's one of the easiest corners in Vegas to build a full night around.

Yes — a free tram connects all three. It runs Excalibur → Luxor → Mandalay Bay and back, so you can park once (or arrive by rideshare) and reach any of the eight shows without driving. There's also a covered indoor walkway between Luxor and Mandalay Bay if you'd rather stroll.

Across the three hotels there are 8 shows we track — Michael Jackson ONE by Cirque du Soleil at Mandalay Bay, Blue Man Group and Carrot Top at Luxor, and a full slate at Excalibur (Tournament of Kings, Mac King, the Australian Bee Gees and Thunder From Down Under). Prices run from $36 to $115.

The Australian Bee Gees Show at Excalibur is the lowest at around $36, followed by Fantasy at Luxor near $40 and Mac King's afternoon comedy-magic show around $50. This end of the Strip has some of the best-value tickets in town.

It's the far south end of the Strip — Excalibur sits on the Tropicana corner across from MGM Grand, with Luxor and Mandalay Bay running south from there. The free tram ties the three together and MGM Grand is a short walk across the intersection.

Michael Jackson ONE at Mandalay Bay is the marquee production — a Cirque du Soleil show built entirely around Michael Jackson's music. Blue Man Group at Luxor is the other headline draw, and Tournament of Kings at Excalibur is the best pick for families.
