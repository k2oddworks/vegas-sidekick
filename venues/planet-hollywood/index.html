The rundown

One of the best-value show clusters on the Strip

Planet Hollywood sits dead-center on the Strip, wrapped by the Miracle Mile Shops — and that mall is quietly one of the densest show districts in Las Vegas. Between the resort's own theaters and the Miracle Mile venues, you can see a magic show, a tribute concert, an adult revue or a family variety spectacular without ever crossing the street.

Most of the shows play the Miracle Mile theater complex — the V Theater, the V2 room upstairs, the V3 Theater and the Saxe Theater — where tickets routinely start in the $30s. The bigger draws have their own rooms: Criss Angel MINDFREAK at the Criss Angel Theater and Chippendales at the Chippendales Theatre inside the main resort. If you want a lot of show for a little money, this is the address.

Planet Hollywood shows — FAQ

Planet Hollywood and its Miracle Mile Shops theaters host 13 shows we track — from Criss Angel MINDFREAK and VEGAS! The Show to value picks like Las Vegas LIVE, Allstars of Magic and Zombie Burlesque. Tickets range from about $29 to $110.

Las Vegas LIVE is usually the lowest at around $29, with Allstars of Magic and Popovich Comedy Pet Theater close behind near $31–$34 — Planet Hollywood is one of the best-value show clusters on the Strip.

Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino sits in the center of the Las Vegas Strip at 3667 S Las Vegas Blvd, wrapped by the Miracle Mile Shops — walking distance to the Bellagio fountains, Paris and the Cosmopolitan.

Most of the shows play in the Miracle Mile Shops theater complex — the V Theater, V2 (upstairs), V3 and Saxe Theater. Criss Angel MINDFREAK has its own Criss Angel Theater, and Chippendales plays the Chippendales Theatre inside the main resort.

The smaller theater shows often have same-week availability, but the headline draws (Criss Angel, Chippendales, VEGAS! The Show) and weekend dates sell through — booking a few days ahead locks the better seats and the lower price.
